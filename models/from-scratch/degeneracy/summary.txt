DEGENERACY CENSUS: trees, DAGs and mixers of the champion league (cycles/champ), 2026-11-09
Question: did the routed models and mixtures degenerate (router to flat, mixer to flat, mixer to router, converged leaves)?

WHAT WAS MEASURED
- Models: each lineage's latest version (copied from cycles/runs; ratings from state.json, cycle 71): 64 routed or
  mixture lineages (active, retired and frozen), plus 9 flat references as context rows. A second pass measured each
  lineage's champion version (column "at champion").
- Positions: models/from-scratch/modelopt/games/round0 (cycle-70 pool sample: 2,983 games, 256,074 positions). Usage
  counts every position; predictions are compared on every 10th (25,608).
- Usage. Routers (trees, DAGs, Hoeffding trees, and the hard MoEs, which predict with their argmax expert): share of
  positions per leaf, "max" share, Neff = 1/sum(p^2), leaves with >= 1%. Soft mixers: the top expert's mean weight
  ("w") and argmax share ("arg"), share of positions whose max weight is >= .9, effective experts per position ("eff",
  exp of the mean weight entropy).
- Similarity. "top": correlation, over the 25,608 positions, of the whole model's white-win minus black-win log-odds
  with those of its dominant part alone (the busiest leaf, or the expert with the highest mean weight). "off": the same,
  on the positions routed to another leaf or expert only. "w": median correlation of win-minus-loss weights between
  LR parts with >= 1% share, over the 819 features F-L-c uses (as whytrees). NB table correlations are in the details.
- Reference bar: F-L-c and F-P-tau0-c (flat LR and flat probit) correlate at 0.9922 on these positions.

VERDICT RULES (in order)
- router to flat: one leaf (never split, or merged back), or the busiest leaf gets >= 95% of positions. Observed:
  99.0 to 100%, against 90.5% for the next highest.
- converged leaves: shares spread, but "top" >= 0.9922, so swapping the whole for its busiest leaf changes predictions
  less than swapping LR for probit. Observed: 0.9937 to 1.0000, against at most 0.9908 for the rest.
- mixer to flat: a soft mixture with "top" >= 0.9922. "flat by weight": the top expert has w >= .8 and arg >= .9, but
  "top" is below the bar.
- mixer to router: max weight >= .9 on >= 75% of positions, and no expert is argmax on >= 90% of them.
- healthy router or mixer: anything else. "dominated": the busiest leaf has >= 50% of positions (mixers: top expert
  w >= .5). "borderline": "top" is within 0.002 of the bar.

HEAD-TO-HEAD (last column)
- The dominant part, saved as a standalone model, plays the whole model of the same version. A match is played when
  the part has >= 50% share or the verdict is flat, converged or router. It is skipped ("identical") when the part gives
  the whole model's prediction on >= 99.99% of positions.
- The league's method: each side calibrated on its own play (600 d1 games, seed 5, starting from the lineage's
  held-out set; the whole model reuses the league's own games from cycles/champ/owncal where kept, 28 of 34).
  200 games at depth 2, seed 11, log-odds softmax T 0.1, --use-probs, paired colours, chessai-hybrid4g.
- Elo is part minus whole, with a 95% CI of about ±45. Positive means the part alone is stronger.

PATTERNS

1. Degeneration is common. At the latest versions, 25 of 64 lineages are functionally flat (14 converged leaves,
   8 router to flat, 3 mixer to flat). Two more mixers are flat by weight, and one mixer became a router.
   36 are healthy (33 routers, 3 mixers), and 12 of those are dominated by one part. At the champion versions,
   31 were functionally flat.

2. The recipe decides it.
   - Leaf sharing makes near-copies. Every LR-leaf tree with --leaf-share converged: Ne-share3, Le-share3, Nc-share
     and Nc-share-l10, with LR weights at 0.992 to 0.999. Ne-share is borderline at 0.9908. NB leaves with share .1
     stay apart (T-N-Nc 0.969, T-N-Nc-fresh 0.910) unless the tree also merges.
   - Merging (--revise 0) keeps trees at one leaf or a few near-copies. 18 of 20 merge lineages were functionally flat
     at their champion version, 8 of them with a single leaf; 13 still are.
   - LR routers can collapse. Three LR error-routed trees send nearly everything to one leaf: T-L-Le 100%, Le-pre
     99.7%, Le-merge 99.0%. So does T-N-Lc-merge (LR cluster router), at 100%. The other 9 LR-routed trees keep 39
     to 87% in their busiest leaf, and 4 of those have converged leaves.
   - NB-routed LR-leaf trees and DAGs without share or merge stay healthy: 15 lineages, "top" 0.958 to 0.991, busiest
     leaf 22 to 67%. The exception is the DAG D-L-Nc-c: its cluster router sends all but 9 of 256,074 positions to one
     of its 3 leaves. The Hoeffding trees spread widest (Neff 11 and 13).
   - Mixers.
     - LR-gated LR experts lean on one expert: M-L4-L w .90 and M-L8-L .87, argmax 97 to 98%. The leftover weight
       still moves predictions ("top" 0.945 and 0.952). M-L4-L-d2jit keeps two experts (.59 and .35).
     - The LR+probit hybrids fall onto the LR expert. Probit mean weight: .083 (M-LP-L), .008 (d2jit), .00005 (win).
     - The NB-gated M-L4-N became a router. One expert has >= .9 weight on 86% of positions, and its four experts
       split them 12/41/10/37%.
     - The NB-expert mixers (M-N4-L, M-N4-N) stay mixers. Their components are not standalone predictors ("top" 0.03
       and 0.04).

3. Known cases, confirmed.
   - The hard MoEs predict with one expert by design (moe-hard, hybrid-hard). On all 25,608 positions, the whole
     model's output equals its argmax expert's exactly.
     - M-LP-L-hard's LR expert takes 99.1% of positions (router to flat).
     - M-L4-L-hard's expert 0 takes 90.5% (dominated router). Its other experts disagree with expert 0 ("off" 0.40).
   - M-LP-L's gate starves the probit expert. The LR expert has mean weight .917 and argmax 97.4%, and the mixture is
     functionally the LR expert ("top" 0.9997).
   - Consistent with whytrees: the share trees' leaves are near-copies (Nc-share's champion v545 "top" 0.9937;
     Ne-share LR weights 0.995).

4. Strength. Degeneration does not track rating.
   - T-L-Ne-share3-c (1889.9, top of the league) has converged leaves ("top" 0.998), and its busiest leaf alone plays
     the whole level (5 [-41, 51]). 4 of the top 8 lineages are functionally flat. Whatever lifts share3 above F-L-c
     (1818.2) lies in how its leaves are trained, not in routing.
   - Where a model is functionally flat, its dominant part plays like the whole: 18 of 19 such matches are level
     within the CI. The exception is T-N-LNs-merge's leaf, at -56 [-104, -10].
   - Some dominated healthy routers are weaker than their own busiest leaf, so their minor leaves cost strength:
     T-L-LLs 145 [98, 197], M-L4-L-hard 123 [77, 173], T-L-NLs-merge 94 [47, 145], T-N-LLs-merge 63 [18, 111].
   - The whole beats its dominant part in two cases:
     - M-L8-L: -89 [-138, -43]. The minor experts matter despite their 13% of the weight.
     - D-L-Ne-rf16: -63 [-110, -19].
   - Five merge lineages had one leaf or converged leaves at their champion version and have distinct leaves now:
     NLs-merge, T-N-NLs-merge, Le-merge-d2jit, T-N-Ne-merge and T-N-LLs-merge. They sit 144 to 210 below their
     champions; the other merge lineages sit between -64 and 16. Large drops also happen without a structure change
     (T-L-Le -182, M-L4-L -186, T-N-Lc-merge -140), so structure is not the whole story.

CAVEATS
- One position set (league positions from the cycle-70 pool). The depth-2 search also visits other positions.
- 200-game matches have a CI of about ±45. Across 34 matches, about 2 chance results are expected at 95%.
- NB tables are a poor similarity measure because rare values dominate their correlation. T-N-NLs-merge's leaf tables
  correlate at 0.998, while their log-odds correlate at 0.78 on positions routed elsewhere. For NB, use "top" and
  "off".
- "peak" is the highest entry rating with CI <= 30 in ratings.csv. Retired and idle entries keep frozen ratings that
  run about 25 to 40 above today's field, so old peaks are inflated. "Elo now" for retired or frozen lineages is from
  the cycle shown.
