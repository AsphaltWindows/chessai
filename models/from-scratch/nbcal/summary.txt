Calibrated self-play policy for NB (topic 3) (2026-11-07). Verdict: skipped after the pre-check, with nothing built.
A calibration refit on the lineage's own games leaves the self-play move choice about as random as it is now. The
policy changes only under a calibration fitted on low-draw games from outside the loop, and that shifts the draw prior
rather than fixing NB's overconfidence.

Pre-check (policy.sh, then the self-play-game calibration by hand into policy-selfcal.out):
- policy-stats plays 200 games with the self-play policy (jitter10, depth 0) and scores every position's candidate
  moves: the gap between the top two expected scores, and how often jitter10 plays a non-top move.
- Models: F-N's recipe from scratch (F-N-s11 at v50, v300, v1000), and F-L-s21 v1000 as the LR reference.
  chessai-tangate, seed 1.

Reading:
- Refit on its own games, as a per-round self-play calibration would be (self-play draws about 87% of games and
  95% of positions), NB's gaps grow 40-70x, to LR's size. But jitter10's ±10% of the expected score still swamps
  them: 87-89% non-top, against 84% now. LR is at 86-87% too. The data policy hardly changes, so the run would be
  F-N-c again.
- Only a calibration fitted on d2 games makes the policy less random (53-55% non-top, shorter games). It does that
  by moving the draw probability from about 0.97 to 0.17, a different draw prior, not by fixing confidence. It also
  works the same way on LR.
- That calibration would bring other models' games into the self-play loop, which the self-play-only rule excludes.
- It is also a "less noise" policy, and less noise lost for LR: T=0.3 log-odds softmax, about 70% non-top, gave
  −200 (−213 to −188) at v1000 d2 against F-L in batch 38.
- So nothing is proposed, for F-N or for F-P.
