TAN gate + LR experts (tangate, the proposed MoE-L-T-c) (2026-11-07).

Verdict: broken as a candidate, so no smoke and no league line. Offline it is clearly worse than MoE-c's recipe and
no better than the NB gate (nbgate, MoE-L-N-c in the restart). The NB gate fails because naive-Bayes posteriors are
extreme, which makes the gate nearly hard. TAN does not remove that: the TAN gate is as hard.

Code: ../tangate-src = ../tanroute-src + tangate.patch.
- src/tangate.rs (TanGateMoe): nbgate with a TAN gate (fixed m, --tan-m) in place of its CNBP. Everything else is
  nbgate's: the hash warm-up (--nbmix-warm), EM responsibilities, responsibility-weighted LR expert steps, and the gate
  counting responsibilities.
- While training, the gate scores from its current counts with the tree of its last refresh. It relearns its tree
  each round.
- Model file: gate tables + experts. State file: every count, for resume.
- Wired into train (from scratch or --start a state), self-play (fresh and --resume from states), every model spec
  (tangate:PATH) and mix-stats.
- nbmix.rs: 5 helpers made pub(crate), no other change. Tan::is_trainable added.

Binary: ../bin/chessai-tangate (md5 bc6fad3c9bbfa005bd1be1b70f152b93), a superset of chessai-tanroute2.
- Identity against chessai-tanroute2, 0 non-log diff lines on both:
  - ../probit/identity/run2.sh (out2-tangate against out2-tanroute2);
  - the TAN-node script ../tanroute/identity/run.sh (out-tangate against out-tanroute2): eval-loss of TAN-router
    trees, self-play with TAN routers from scratch and resumed (with merges), and train.
- Unit tests: 132 pass. The 4 new ones check:
  - two experts fit what one cannot;
  - one expert equals nbgate's one expert exactly;
  - before its first refresh the gate scores as nbgate's CNBP, within 1e-6;
  - the text round trip, and the state resume exact.

Gate hardness (mix-stats on RR 84): 3.97 of 4 experts used, the largest weight over 0.99 in 63.7% of positions, and

Training cost: 70 CPU-s on the 2.3M positions, against 15 for nbgate.
- Rounds after the first refresh take 43 s against 4.3: the gate's count-based scoring with parents reads the pair
  counts at random, for 4 classes.
- So it is also about 5x nbgate's training cost a position. If it were ever wanted, a table refresh every 1024
  examples would give nbgate's staleness at a fraction of the cost.
