Leaf sharing (--leaf-share) for LR-leaf trees (2026-11-07). This was the extra idea; the ledger has sharing for
CNBP leaves only.

Verdict: it works, and it is a league candidate as T-L-Ne-share-c against T-L-Ne-c (no merges).
- Every leaf also takes a gradient step on 0.1 times the log-loss of the examples routed to the other leaves.
- Without merges this makes LR error trees clearly better offline, and in self-play it shrinks the split's loss to
  its shadow and halves the seams.
- With merges it changes nothing: splits are still merged back. So no share-merge lineage.

Code: ../lrshare-src = ../tangate-src + lrshare.patch.
- In Graph::train_leaf, sharing now also covers LR leaves, with an AdaGrad step on share x log-loss from the leaf's own
  prediction. CNBP leaves are unchanged.
- The CLI doc is updated, and there is a new unit test: sharing keeps LR leaves closer (133 tests pass).

- At v20 both no-merge Ne trees have 11 leaves. The leaf-pair log-odds difference is 0.35 with sharing against
  0.84 without.
- Early in training the split still loses to the shadow on average, with or without sharing. Sharing cuts that loss
  by 40-60%, so with merges everything still merges.
- CPU, user-s for 20 rounds:
  - ne: 68.4 with sharing against 50.6, 35% more;
  - nc: 58.0 against 52.1, 11% more;
  - all of the extra is training, since every example trains every leaf (11 leaves at the end). Play is unchanged.
