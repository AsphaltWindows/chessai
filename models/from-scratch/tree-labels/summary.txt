WHAT
  grow7 (fastbin/grow7-src; = hybrid6 on the standard's capped trees, = grow6 on grow trees and DAGs) with search
  labels for trees and DAGs: `train --model bdt|bdd --label-depth K --label-result W [--label-model SPEC]`.
  Each position's label = W x the game's result + (1 - W) x the outcome a depth-K search expects from it, the search
  by the tree as it starts the round (its own leaves evaluate the search's leaves), or by --label-model's model,
  calibrated on the round's results (selfplay::fit_calibration / search_labels, as the flat path). The blended label
  goes into Bdt::train / Bdd::train like a result label, so leaves AND routers' error targets train on it; splits
  and growth logic unchanged. A tree that scores rounds (--log-fresh-gains, --revise-on fresh) gets observe_round /
  train_in_game / end_round with the blended labels and its games' ids, as train_rounds gives them. Only main.rs
  changed (tree-labels/src; diff against fastbin/grow7-src).
