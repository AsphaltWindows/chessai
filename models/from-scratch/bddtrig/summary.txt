bddtrig: non-merging error-style router splits on T-L-Ne-c's recipe, one factor at a time (2026-11-08)
Arms: C alone (router-split copies forget 16x), a skill trigger and a 2-SE margin, each against the plain BDD and the
BDT with the same leaves; plus a routing-headroom check and stats on the live D-L-Ne-c's router split.
Setup as bddstudy/bddfix: LR leaves, CNBP error routers, --route error --error log, branch 3, forget 1, no --revise;
offline-c15 RRs 80-83, 60 rounds, seeds 1 and 2, split threshold 100k, limit 10; held out on RR 84 with cross-fold
calibration, paired t over 10 parts.

VERDICT
No trigger beats a BDT with the same leaves. C alone and the skill trigger end level with it, and both end better
than the plain BDD; the 2-SE margin changes nothing. Across five studies and seven variants, router splits have never
beaten a same-leaves BDT offline. Nothing new to stage: D-L-Ne-skill-c (chessai-bddtrig2) and D-L-Ne-rf16-c (C alone)
are in the cycle 43->44 batch, so the league will judge both.

STEP 0, HEADROOM: could any router do better with these leaves?
Per leaf, a ridge regression of the leaf's raw log-loss on the one-hot features (852 inputs, intercept unpenalized);
each position goes to the leaf with the lowest predicted loss. Trees: BDT-3 and BDT-4 at round 60, both seeds. Raw
log-loss per position on RR 84, ridge minus own routing:
- Fitted on the training rounds (RRs 80-83, 2.3M positions), lambda 100: BDT-3 0.0015 [-0.0031, 0.0062] and 0.0078
  [0.0046, 0.0110]; BDT-4 0.0013 [-0.0031, 0.0058] and 0.0075 [0.0047, 0.0104]. Lambda 10000 gives 0.0016 and 0.0063
  for BDT-3. This fit favours own routing: each leaf trained on the positions routed to it.
- Fitted on one half of RR 84 and scored on the other: 0.015 to 0.021 worse (lambda 10 to 1000). In sample it beats
  own by 0.027 to 0.031: a half holds about 3.5k games, and a game's positions share an outcome, so it overfits.
- Fresh routers refitted on one half (CNBP or LR, Best target, 4 passes), calibrated: 0.009 to 0.050 worse.
- For scale: own routing is 0.019 to 0.025 better than the best single leaf. The per-position oracle is 0.25 to 0.29
  better than own routing, but it uses the outcome.
Read: no linear routing gain is visible on these leaves. That is weak evidence, not a bound: one fit favours own
routing and the other overfits.

STEP 1, C ALONE: --router-forget-factor 16 on chessai-bddfix
The copies a router split makes divide their counts by 16, so they part at once; leaf-split copies keep
--forget-factor 1. Nothing else changes.
- Structure: s1 6 router / 4 leaf splits, root split in rounds 4, 7, 9 and 11, 9 leaves; s2 7 / 3, root in 4, 6, 8
  and 11, 7 leaves. The plain BDD: 6 / 4 in both seeds, root 3 or 4 times, 9 leaves.
- C caused bddfix's early damage. At the end of the split's round the siblings agree on 78% and 71% of test positions
  (the BDD's copies: 96% and 94%). Against the no-split BDT at round 4, root regret is 0.0157 [0.0127, 0.0187] and
  0.0327 [0.0276, 0.0378] higher, and tree loss 0.0021 [0.0000, 0.0042] and 0.0080 [0.0047, 0.0114] higher. By round
  8 the tree-loss gap is 0.0022 [-0.0016, 0.0059] and 0.0003 [-0.0020, 0.0026].
- The damage passes: worse than the same-leaves BDT at round 20, level at 40 and 60.

STEP 2A, SKILL TRIGGER: --delegator-split-on skill on chessai-bddtrig2
A router's window statistic is the loss of the child it routes to minus that of the child with the smallest summed
loss since the node got its children (the best single child so far); below zero, it beats the best single child.
It splits by --split-rule, here Worse with margin 0: when a window's mean rises above the previous window's.
- Bug, fixed: the first build (chessai-bddtrig) read any negative window as "no previous window" (the sentinel is -1
  and the test was previous >= 0). Skill windows are mostly negative, so it almost never split a router: skill-s1
  matched BDT-10. chessai-bddtrig2 tests previous != -1 (unit test added). The runs were redone, and D-L-Ne-skill-c
  was restaged on the fixed binary before deploy.
- Structure: 7 router / 3 leaf splits in both seeds, 7 leaves; root split in rounds 5, 7 and 8 (s1), 5, 7 and 13 (s2).
- The first split comes one window later than the BDD's (round 5) and costs nothing. The copies agree on 96% and 95%,
  and the tree-loss gap to the no-split BDT is -0.0003 [-0.0008, 0.0001] and -0.0008 [-0.0018, 0.0002]. At round 8
  it is -0.0005 [-0.0022, 0.0011] and -0.0051 [-0.0077, -0.0025].
- Defect: after a split, an R' node's children are copies that route alike, so its skill windows sit at zero (-0.000
  then 0.000 nats before s1's second root split, -0.000 then -0.001 before its third). Margin 0 then splits on noise,
  bddregret's A defect again. Skill with a margin would stop that (--router-split-z applies to it too). Not run: the
  plan stayed lean.

STEP 2B, MARGIN: --router-split-z 2 on chessai-bddtrig
A router split needs its window to rise by more than 2 SE (per position, from the windows' squared errors); leaf
splits are unchanged. Both seeds are byte-identical to bddstudy's plain BDD through round 60 (all 60 model files),
though the state records the option. Positions in a game are correlated, so a per-position SE is small; a game-level
SE might bite, but the climb the trigger fires on is systematic either way. Withdrawn from the league batch.

STEP 3, LIVE D-L-Ne-c: bdd-stats on v92, v93 (the root's router split) and v140
Scored on RR 84 (575k positions) and on own-step calibration games of v131 and v140 (73k positions).
- R' is a pass-through. At v93 the copies agree on 100% and R' equals its busiest copy (0.0000). At v140 they agree on
  96.9% (RR 84) and 97.0% (own games); R' minus its busiest copy is 0.0000 [-0.0002, 0.0002] and -0.0003. With
  forget 1 the copies keep their counts and drift apart slowly, as in bddstudy.
- Root base regret climbs, as offline: 0.4844, 0.5004 and 0.6304 on RR 84, 0.1938, 0.2008 and 0.2361 on own games
  (v92, v93, v140). Tree raw loss on own games: 0.9787, 1.0059, 0.9771. Routing is identical at v92 and v93, so the
  v93 rise is that round's leaf training, not the split.
- By v140 leaf 1 has split too (node 1 over leaves 7, 8, 9): 3 of the 5 splits are used.
