Why the switch trees can't be judged fully offline:
- Offline, no delegator of any switch recipe switches to its error router (as eloss found: the error router's gain is
  never positive on this benchmark). So T-L-NLs and T-L-NNs train the same tree (error routers aren't saved in the
  model file), and the check measures only their cluster-routed phase. Sharing there acts like it did on nc-s01
  (-0.0010 [-0.0023, 0.0003], lrshare).
- In the league they did switch: T-L-NLs-c 2 delegators, T-L-NNs-c 1, T-L-LLs-c 1 ("switched to error routing" in
  cycles/runs/<lineage>.log). Error-routed delegators are where sharing helped most offline (T-L-Ne -0.0058 at 0.1,
  T-L-Le -0.0063 at 0.3), so this check probably understates sharing for the league's switch trees.
- T-L-LLs with LR cluster routers is flat LR offline (lls-0 - lr: 0.0000 [-0.0001, 0.0000]); its 7 leaves predict
  alike, so sharing has nothing to act on.
