b-hard (blendsp, pure self-play, no own steps) is unaffected.

Verdict: it helps mature lineages and costs young ones. Proposed as driver-side entries for existing lineages; no
new training lineage.
- Mature lineages, averaged entries against single versions:
  - T-L-Le-c (league): 40 at ply 1, every window positive; -11 at ply 2 (not significant).
  - T-L-Ne-c (league): 7 at ply 1; 28 at ply 2.
  - b-hard v40-v60: 21 at ply 1, 45 at ply 2. Held-out loss -0.005, with round-to-round swings a fifth as large.
- Young run (the binary's smoke, v22-v26 of a fresh run): -15 at ply 1, -1 at ply 2. The average lags a model that
  is still improving fast.
- Cost: nothing in training. Averaging the files takes about a second a version.

Why:
- Topic 3 found adjacent versions far apart. Head to head, b-hard v42 beats v43 by 74 at ply 1 and 177 at ply 2, and
  by 65 and 171 with the same held-out calibration for both.
- v43's held-out log-loss spikes by 0.024 and recovers at v44.
- From one version to the next, the LR routers move up to a third of the positions between subtrees.
- Averaging the weights of the last few iterates (Polyak-Ruppert) is the textbook cure for noisy SGD iterates.

Method (avg.py; bdtparse.py is splice.py's parser):
- For files of the same structure, every logistic-regression node (leaves and routers) gets the mean of its weights.
  AdaGrad sums, CNBP counts and the node headers come from the last file.
- Flat LR files are averaged the same way.
- avg5(N) is the mean of versions N-4..N. A window that crosses a split is skipped; none did here.

In training (chain.sh -> chain.out; smoke/run.sh -> smoke/run.out): bin/chessai-polyak, md5

- ../polyak-src = ../playrouter-src + polyak.patch.
- What --polyak DECAY does, BDT self-play only:
  - each LR node (leaves, routers, play and switch routers) keeps an exponential moving average of its weights,
    moved once a round, after training;
  - model files carry the averages in the weights' place;
  - prediction, training, self-play and the state's weights are unchanged, and the state keeps the averages;
  - a resumed run without the flag drops them.
- Children of a split inherit their parent's average. New routers start theirs at their first round.
- State: an LR node with an average is written under a new tag (4). Old states, and states without averages, are
  unchanged.
- Two new unit tests (averages move by the decay, fill the files, and survive the state; training is unchanged).
  144 tests pass.
- Identity against chessai-playrouter without the flag: 0 non-log diff lines on all four identity scripts.
- Smoke, scaled-down T-L-Le, seed 21: plain ran 26 rounds; avg ran 20 rounds with --polyak 0.8, then was resumed to
  26.
  - The ladders are identical. The v1 files are identical, since the first average is the weights. The v26 files
    differ, with the same 16 nodes.
  - chessai-sparsefit scores the averaged file exactly as chessai-polyak does.
  - Held-out loss, v18-v26: level on average (avg 0.8244, plain 0.8212 at v26).
  - Cal-own arenas over v22-v26: -15 (-27 to -4) at ply 1, -1 (-25 to 23) at ply 2.
- Reading: in a young run the EMA's lag (mean age 4 rounds) costs more than the noise it removes, and the averages
  that children inherit at splits are stale.
- Restarting the averages at splits would be cleaner, but it doesn't matter for mature lineages, whose trees have
  stopped splitting.

Proposal (for the driver, no new binary):
- For a mature lineage, enter averaged versions beside, or instead of, the raw ones.
  - Command: python3 models/from-scratch/polyak/avg.py OUT bdt{N-4}.model ... bdtN.model (oldest first).
  - It averages the LR weights and leaves out files from before a split.
  - Its output is an ordinary BDT file, so the tournament binary is unchanged.
- One factor from the lineage's own entries, at no training cost.
- First candidates:
  - T-L-Le-c: 40 (30 to 51) at ply 1, positive in all five windows;
  - T-L-Ne-c: 28 (2 to 55) at ply 2.
- Not proposed: from-scratch lineages with --polyak (the smoke's lag cost).

CPU: series and league held-out evaluations about 4 CPU-min; arenas (b-hard, league) about 10; EMA series 2; build,
identity and smoke about 8. About 24 CPU-min.

Not run (stopped for topic spdrop): avg.py has a diagnostic mode, `avg.py --only leaves|routers OUT FILE...`.
- It averages only the leaves' or only the routers' LR weights; every other node comes from the last file.
- It is meant to split the Polyak gain between leaves and routers ("leaves bind").
- The default mode is unchanged: on b-hard v39-v43 the output is byte-identical to avg/b43.model.
- No arenas or held-out runs used it.
- Caveat: avg.py writes weights with 6 significant digits. A full-precision round trip would need repr(), as
  spdrop/interp.py does.


Window check (batch 2, the coordinator's fork, 2026-11-08 day; window/run.sh -> window/run.out, window/pool.py ->

A run's versions alternate: each cycle's self-play versions, then its own-step version (E+1, when the gate keeps it),
from which the next cycle's self-play starts. spdrop found the two phases far apart at ply 2 and ply 1 for T-L-Le.

- Inside one cycle's self-play, averaging gains nothing for today's T-L-Le-c at either ply. Its adjacent self-play
  versions don't differ enough for the average to help.
- A window that ends on the own-step version is a phase mixture: four self-play versions pull the average to the
  self-play phase, which is 96 stronger at ply 1 and 102 weaker at ply 2 than the own-step version. That is the spdrop
  swing again, measured on weights.
- So 4d's "40 at ply 1, -11 at ply 2" for T-L-Le-c was mostly phase, not noise reduction.

For the decision held for the user (Polyak entries):
- No case for T-L-Le-c. Any averaged entry must keep its window inside one cycle's self-play versions, or it measures
  the phase swing.
- T-L-Ne-c's 28 at ply 2 (4d) needs the same check before it counts; its windows were in the same version era.
- The champion-stabiliser argument rested on version noise between adjacent versions. In today's mature T-L-Le-c,
  that noise doesn't show within a cycle; the big swings are between the phases.
