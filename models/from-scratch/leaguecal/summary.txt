leaguecal: does the league's fixed 0.1-nat softmax confound ratings through each entry's calibration?
Diagnostic only, no league change. 2026-11-07. Tournament binary bin/chessai-tanroute2
(md5 a25836325c9571bdd1b88aa227f450e1); nothing new was built.

IN SHORT
- Yes, the confound is real and large. On its own play, each entry's league spec has a calibration slope
  between 0.55 and 2.03 (median 1.16). The league spec is cal:HELDOUT, fitted on held-out d2 games.
  - Softmax at T over a spec with own-play slope a plays like softmax at a*T in the entry's calibrated units.
  - So d0-trained LR entries (median slope 1.5-1.7) play hotter than their own calibration says.
  - NB entries (0.86) and d2-trained entries (0.93) play colder.
  - Colder won at T 0.1 for every model tested.
- Measured directly, head to head at ply 2: 132 Elo per unit ln(slope ratio), 95% 96 to 169.
  - Between a d0 LR tree (slope 1.65-1.80) and a d2-trained entry (0.73-0.88), that is 90-113 Elo.
- It changes the order at the top of the seeding field, but it does not reverse it.
  - Now the first 9 ply-2 entries are all d2-trained, and the best d0 LR tree is 81 below the top.
  - With every entry on cal-own, the best estimate puts the top 3 within about 30 Elo:
    F-P-tau0-d2jit-c-v2036, T-L-Nc-merge-c-v1631, F-L-d2jit-c-v1651.
  - The top 15 would be mixed: 6 d2-trained, 4 LR trees, 4 flat LR, 1 MoE.
  - Among the family means, d2-trained falls from level with the d0 LR families to 65-90 below them.
  - In the two pairs played head to head, the d2-trained side stays ahead even under cal-own, by 24 and 28.
- Fair alternative, cheapest of those that keep the current policy: cal-own.
  - Calibrate each version on 600 of its own ply-1 games (self-play only) and play cal:OWNGAMES:KIND:PATH.
  - Cost: 1.2-3.1 CPU-s per version, measured on one thread. About 1.5 CPU-min per cycle for 54 new entries.
  - The spec form already works in the tournament binary.
  - The scale-free alternative (argmax or rank-based softmax with random openings) costs nothing per version
    but was not tested.

METHOD
1. Survey (survey.py → survey.csv, survey_summary.txt)
   - Field: 103 unique models from champion-sim/stage2/dry-run.out, the restart's seeding field:
     54 latest versions and 49 old peaks, references left out.
   - League spec per model: cal:HELDOUT:KIND:PATH, as stage2 cycle.py builds it. HELDOUT is lr or cnbp, from
     lineages.txt and config.json.
   - Own games: the league spec against itself, 600 games, league policy (log-odds softmax, T 0.1), ply 1,
     seed 5. Stored in games/NAME.
   - Fit: eval-loss SPEC games/NAME --use-probs --fit-calibration, once for the league spec and once for the raw
     model. "Slope" below means slope_winner of the league spec's fit. Under softmax only the slope matters,
     because the offset difference is the same for every child.
2. Effect size: cal-own against the league spec of the same model (step2.sh, step2b.sh → step3.py → step3.txt)
   - cal-own = cal:games/NAME:KIND:PATH, the raw model calibrated on its own 600 games.
   - Ply 1: 1000 games per model. Ply 2: 600 games per model. Several seeds, alternating colours.
3. Direct check of the predicted re-ranking (step4.sh → step5.py → step5.txt)
   - Two pairs, 600 games each at ply 2.
   - Played once with both sides on their league specs and once with both sides on cal-own.

ANSWERS
- Would the confound change the order among the top entries? Yes.
  - The current d2-trained sweep of the top 9 is partly sharpness.
  - With cal-own the top is mixed:
    - the best d0 LR tree climbs from 12th to about 2nd;
    - MoE-d2jit-c-v2156 drops from 4th to about 17th;
    - F-L-d2play-c-v1011 drops from 8th to about 20th.
  - It does not reverse the top. The top 3 come within about 30 Elo of each other.
  - Both d2-trained entries tested still win head to head under cal-own, by 24 and 28.
- Would it change the order between families? Yes, on the means.
  - Now d2-trained (1610) is level with flat LR (1636) and LR trees (1617).
  - Under cal-own it falls 65-90 below them.
  - At the top, d2-trained's lead over the best d0 LR tree shrinks from 81 to about 15.
  - Whether d2 training "beats" d0 training is therefore mostly a scale effect in today's ratings, except at the
    very top.
- NB/CNBP stays last either way. Its overconfidence gives it a small free boost now, about 35 Elo on the mean,
  and possibly more: its self-arena below slope 1 was steeper.

FAIR ALTERNATIVES AND COST PER VERSION
(a) cal-own, recommended if the league keeps the T 0.1 softmax.
    - Each version plays 600 games of itself at ply 1 (league spec or raw). The league then uses
      cal:OWNGAMES:KIND:PATH.
    - It is self-play only, zero knowledge, no hand features.
    - Measured cost on one thread (step4.out), 600 games plus one fit:
      - flat LR F-L-c-v2860: 1.33 + 0.11 CPU-s;
      - tree T-L-Nc-merge-c-v1631: 1.14 + 0.07 CPU-s;
      - MoE-d2jit-c-v2156: 2.87 + 0.21 CPU-s.
    - About 1.5 CPU-min per cycle for 54 new entries, one per lineage.
    - The survey's cpu_s column is about 2x too high: it counted child time across two worker threads. The real
      survey total was 300 CPU-s for 103 models with two fits each.
    - Disk: the games dir is about 11 MB per version. Keep it while the version is in the league, since the spec
      refits at load (0.07-0.2 s per load). Storing the 4 fitted numbers would need a new spec form.
    - The 103 own-game dirs in games/ are exactly the seeding field's cal-own inputs, so a restart could use them
      at no extra cost.
    - What it equalizes: T 0.1 then means the same in outcome units for every entry. An entry still plays sharper
      if it really separates moves better on its own play.
(b) Scale-free policy: argmax or a rank-based softmax, with randomized openings for variety.
    - Zero cost per version, and ratings would measure move order only.
    - It changes play for everyone and loses the variety T 0.1 gives. Not tested here.
(c) Fit on each entry's own league games instead of its own self-play.
    - About 0.1-0.2 s per fit and no extra games.
    - It needs the league to keep its games per entry, lags one round, and mixes in the opponents' play.
- Not checked: whether self-play for training uses the same held-out spec under a softmax. If it does, the
  effective self-play temperature also differs by family.

CORRECTIONS TO EARLIER REPORTS (errwhy)
- "Recalibrating on its own games gives T-L-Le-c 56 Elo at ply 2 (own-game slope about 1.35)": that 56 was
  measured against the raw model, not the league spec. The league spec is already calibrated on held-out d2 games.
  - Against the league spec, the own-game gain for T-L-Le-fresh-c-v742 (league-spec slope 1.42) is 69 at ply 2
    and 29 at ply 1.
  - The conclusion holds; only the comparator changes.
- The blend-play variants' arena gains were measured against raw hard routing.
  - Part of those gains is the own-game calibration's sharpening: 29 at ply 1 and 69 at ply 2 over the league
    spec for T-L-Le-fresh-c-v742.
  - The blend variants should be compared with cal-own of the same model before they are judged.

CAVEATS
- The slopes are fitted on ply-1 own games, while the first-run ratings and the shifts are ply 2.
- The re-ranking assumes a head-to-head shift carries over to rating and scales with ln(slope).
  - Its single coefficient comes from 2 pairs, both a d0 LR tree against a d2-trained entry.
  - Entries far from that range (NB at 0.55) may shift more.
- The first-run ratings carry error bars of 7-79 in dry-run.out (median 43). Orders inside about 40 Elo are
  not settled, by them or by this estimate.
