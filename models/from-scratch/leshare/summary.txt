Topic 1 (2026-11-07/08 night): leaf sharing (--leaf-share) on the T-L-Le recipe (LR error routers, no merges).

Verdict: it works, as it did for T-L-Ne. It is a league candidate as T-L-Le-share-c against T-L-Le-c.
- Offline, sharing 0.1 brings the LR-router error tree from clearly worse than flat LR to level with it.
- Sharing 0.3 is better still offline.
- In self-play it is not broken: the split's loss to its shadow shrinks by a quarter, seams by a quarter, and the
  leaves agree more.

Reading:
- Error routing makes leaves specialise harder, so they need more borrowed data.
- Cluster leaves are broader already, and more sharing only pulls them towards the flat model.
- Near the optimum the curve is flat (0.1 to 0.3 differ by about 0.001), so a per-leaf schedule could add little.
