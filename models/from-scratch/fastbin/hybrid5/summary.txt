HYBRID5: EXACT FIXED-POINT PLAY FOR TREES AND DAGS (2026-11-09 23:59 to 2026-11-10 ~01:15, the hybrid5 fork)

Task: make play of compound models (trees, DAGs, mixtures) much cheaper with exactly the same results, and swap it
into the running experiments. The user has said yes to any exact, deterministic speedup.

RESULT
- Binary: models/from-scratch/bin/chessai-hybrid5 (md5 3154f5a990cf524c6571aacacf5f6e05).
  - Source: models/from-scratch/fastbin/hybrid5-src, a copy of hybrid4gfx-src.
  - It has the same commands, flags and files as chessai-hybrid4gfx.
- It is the tournament binary of single-split, single-split-probit and cluster-error, from cycles 112, 123 and 40.
- Live tournament CPU per game, new / old: 0.37 (single-split), 0.59 (single-split-probit), 0.49 (cluster-error).
  Own calibration: 0.87-0.96. Cycles finish 20-36% sooner. Details under LIVE below.

HOW IT WORKS
- Only calibrated trees and DAGs at ply 1 and 2 take the new path: `cal:` over bdt or bdd, with no per-leaf
  calibration or blend. Everything else is unchanged: training, self-play, flat models, mixtures (moe, nbgate) and FM.
- Each node keeps its sums in fixed point, with weights at 2^-32. A move updates only the features it changes. TAN
  routers also update the features whose parent changed.
- A router picks its class from the integer sums when its margin is wider than a proven rounding bound. Inside the
  bound, it routes in float.
- Ply 1: the route is fixed point. The leaf's value is computed in float, so it is the exact float value.
- Ply 2:
  - Every reply gets a fixed-point estimate of its leaf value, with a proven error bound.
  - Probit leaves get a bound per position. Outside |z| < 30 they are valued in float.
  - Only replies that could still be the best, given the bounds, are valued in float. That is almost always one per
    move.
  - The float search loop then runs over those exact values in legal-move order, with the same tie and NaN rules.
- So every value that reaches the move choice is the float value, bit for bit.

- 155 unit tests pass. The 6 new ones cover:
  - exact values with LR, NB and TAN routers and LR, NB and probit leaves;

CAVEATS
- Ply 1 gains less: 0.7-0.9 for trees and 0.43 for DAGs. The leaf value is still computed in float for every move.
- Own calibration gains only 4-13%. It plays at ply 1, and fitting the calibration at load is a fixed cost.
- Mixtures (moe, nbgate) and FM are not done. They play the float path, unchanged.
- The second priority was not started: training speedups and probit self-play.
- CPU used: about 40 CPU min, at nice 19. That covers the builds, the checks, the benchmarks and the in-situ replay.
