FLAGS (only with --split-rule grow; refused with a --router-split-rule or without the grow rule; defaults = grow3 = grow1)
  --grow-gate {stall|age}       default stall. age: a leaf is eligible once it has closed --grow-age-windows of its own
                                windows (default 3, at least 1, refused under stall). No stagnation test. Credits, trial
                                slots and the doubling back-off are unchanged.
  --grow-priority {excess|snapshot}  default excess. snapshot: the eligible leaf with the SMALLEST last snapshot gain
                                goes first. Leaves without a measurement rank after measured ones. Ties and unmeasured
                                leaves fall back to grow1's order (excess, then n).
  --grow-min-gain X             default 0 (off). A trial is kept iff gain > the z bar, gain > the MDL bar (the MDL bar is
                                0 under --grow-mdl 0) and gain > X. X must be finite and at least 0.
  Paired snapshot gain (on under --grow-gate age or --grow-priority snapshot):
    - At each window close, the leaf's model is copied.
    - Through the next window, every example the leaf scores is also scored by the copy, before the online leaf trains.
    - d = loss(copy) - loss(online), in the --error measure (nats under --error log), the close lines' units.
    - d is blocked in 20 blocks like the window. At the next close the log gets
      "grow snapshot of leaf L at N n: gain +m (block se s over 20 blocks, z ...)" and a fresh copy is made.
    - The first d after a close is exactly 0: the same model and the same predict_training as the leaf's cached output.
    - A trial start or an undo drops the leaf's copy; its next close makes a new one.
    - The copy and its blocks are saved in the state, so resume is exact mid-window.
    - New options enter the state's option string only when set, so grow3's states read and write byte for byte.

CHOICES WHERE THE DIRECTIVE WAS OPEN (the simplest reading)
  - Age counts all of a leaf's own closes, including those before an undone trial: closes persist across the restart,
    as grow1's back-off counts them. After an undo, the back-off and one fresh close govern, so a leaf backed off
    1 window re-enters at its next close (the age-snap smoke's leaf 1 at 21.17M).
  - Snapshots run under either condition the directive logs them for (gate age or priority snapshot), otherwise off.
  - Min-gain 0 is off, not a "> 0" bar, so the default verdict is grow1's exactly.
  - P1 has 112 training rounds (22.4M positions), so the smoke ran to 22.4M, not 25M.
