THE OPTION (train only: play, self-play and the model file format are unchanged)
  --weight-by-uncertainty none|score (default none)   --weight-floor 0.2   --weight-memory 1000000   --weight-cap 3
  Per example: s = P(W) + P(D)/2 from the model's own pre-update prediction (the one each learner computes anyway:
  the probit's latent after its tau dynamics; the LR's softmax). u = clamp(4 s (1 - s), floor, 1);
  n += 1; m += (u - m) / min(n, memory); w = min(u / m, cap). The example's own u enters m first, so the first
  weight is exactly 1.
  F-P: the truncation update's likelihood power alpha_x = alpha * w (the --probit-alpha path). The tau dynamics and
       the eps (draw-margin) step stay unweighted, as they are under --probit-alpha.
  F-L: weights -= rate * (w g) / sqrt(G), with G += g^2 unweighted. The weighted step is scalar; at w = 1 it is
       bit-identical to the AVX2 step (tested).
  Refused, with a clear error and no output: trees, DAGs, MoE and cnbp ("implemented for the flat models lr and
  probit only"), --label-depth, and self-play.
  State: m and n, plus the options, go in {kind}N.state beside the model (87 bytes), written by every weighted
  train. A train with --start X.model reads X.state if it exists. If not, it starts the mean afresh and notes so
  on stderr. A state written with other floor, cap or memory is refused:
    "...probit1.state: the state's weighting options differ from this run's (give the options it was trained with)"
  Weighted model files load in older binaries: hybrid16's eval-loss on a weighted model gives the same numbers.
  Every weighted train prints, after its "round 0: N positions" line (the driver's regex is unaffected):
    weights by uncertainty (score): N examples, mean w .., mean u .., at floor ..%, at cap ..%, running mean m .. (n ..)
  Source changes:
    src/uweight.rs (new): the weight, its running mean and state, the Weighted<M> wrapper.
    src/probit.rs: update_with, which multiplies the power by w.
    src/logreg.rs: train_uw and step_uw.
    src/classifier.rs: a round_report hook, None by default.
    src/lib.rs, src/main.rs: the flags, the checks, the train dispatch and the report line.
  With the flag absent or none, nothing goes through the new code.

  python3 uloss.py A B [--games DIR] [--band 0.3] [--binary BIN] [--block 1000]
    A, B: eval-loss specs (probit:PATH, lr:PATH) or paths named <kind>N.model. A is the reference (unweighted) and
          also defines the unsure subset; B is the contrast.
    --games: a dir of outcome files. Default: heldout-p2 (above).
  Reports both models' full and winner log-loss, overall and on A's unsure subset (|s - 0.5| < band; s = P(W) +
  P(D)/2), and B - A paired with an SE clustered by game. Games come from DIR/games.csv; without it, the SE uses
  blocks of --block positions (conservative, since the files are sorted by result).
  The predictions are eval-loss's own: `chessai-weight1 eval-loss SPEC DIR --dump FIFO`, read through a named pipe
  in a temporary dir that the tool removes itself, so nothing is written to disk. Each model's overall numbers are
  checked against eval-loss's summary line. Pure Python; about 1 s.
