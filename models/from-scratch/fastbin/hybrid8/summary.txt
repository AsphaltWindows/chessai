METHOD (fastprobit.rs; the same method as compound::best for trees in hybrid5)
- A flat probit plays in fixed point (FxLinear, probit head). A position's value for the side to move is the
  calibration's z_win - z_loss of probit::log_probs_of_latent: a sqrt, two divisions, two ln Phi and the draw's log
  (about 70 ns). At depth 2 a root move's value is the smallest of its replies' values (negamax with a full window:
  no cutoffs but at an infinite value), so most replies need not be valued in full.
- Each reply gets an estimate from a table of ln Phi (grid z_k = -30 + k/256, the bits of probit::ln_cdf_first),
  with a proven bound on its distance from FxLinear::value:
  - ln Phi is concave with -1 < (ln Phi)'' < 0, so the chord lies below it by at most h^2/8: chord + h^2/16 is within
    h^2/16;
  - each float ln Phi is allowed 1e-13 + 16u|ln Phi| (u = 2^-53), the allowance compound uses for its rational
    approximations; the interpolation's and x's rounding are added;
  - the rest is compound's bound: the calibration's rounding (8u of the terms' sizes), times 4, plus 1e-10.
  - The z's are computed from the sums with the exact path's operations, so they are the same bits. No estimate
    (|z| > 29.5, a variance <= 1e-12, eps/s <= 1e-6, a bound >= 1e-3): the reply is valued in full.
- Only replies whose lower bound is at most the smallest upper bound are valued in full; negamax's loop then runs
  over them in move order with the same comparisons, so the same value, ties and signed zeros. Known replies (mates,
  draws, repetitions) carry their exact values. If no exact value lies under the smallest upper bound, every reply is
  valued (never seen).
- Moves, hashes, repetition history, node counts and game ends follow child_fx and negamax_fx step for step.
- Switches: CHESSAI_FX_PROBIT2=off gives hybrid7's search; CHESSAI_FX_CHECK=1 asserts every estimate within its
  bound and every root value bit-equal to hybrid7's full search (it also turns on hybrid5's checks for trees and the
  flat LR/NB checks); CHESSAI_FX_STATS=1 prints a "probit2 fx:" line.
- Stats (two F-P@2 entries, 60 games): 9,941 searches, 247,146 root moves, 5,942,398 replies, 0 without an estimate,
  242,586 valued in full (24 beyond one per root move), 0 positions valued all in full. So about 1 reply in 24 is
  valued in full; what is left is move generation, the incremental sums and the estimates.
