WHY THE CAP WAS 64, AND WHY 128 IS SAFE
- No note gives a reason for 64. hybrid5 (11-10, fixed-point play for trees and DAGs) set it without comment
  (hybrid5/summary.txt). hybrid15's summary and the ledger call it "hybrid5's cap": graphs over 64 nodes play in float,
  "exact but at hybrid14's speed". The ~01:00 review (item 2) held raising it as "measure first: fixed-point play at
  64-256 nodes may stop paying". So it was a speed guard, sized when the models had at most 25 nodes. It was never a
  correctness bound.
- In the code (hybrid19-src), MAX_NODES appears only in Tables::new's eligibility check (compound.rs:263). Nothing is
  sized by it, and nothing depends on the node count:
  - Each node has its own fixed-point table (Table::Linear: rows, with u16 starts into its own rows; Table::Tan: u32
    offsets, u128 child masks over the 70 features), its own Sums, its own margin and its own error bound.
  - A sum has at most 71 entries (a bias and 70 features). Each is a weight |w| < 2^20, quantized to units of 2^-32,
    so under 2^52 units; fixed::quantize refuses anything larger. So |sum| < 71 x 2^52 < 2^59 at any node count, and
    an i64 can't overflow.
  - Cell offsets are bounded per route state (|part| < 1e6 in logs). Route states have their own cap (MAX_STATES 4096,
    u32 ids) and hold usize node ids.
  - Acc (per-node Sums) and Tables are Vecs. Routes follow usize child indices. Changes is per feature: [(u8,u8);16]
    and a u128 over the 70 features.
  - The fallback rule is per decision. A router decides in fixed point only outside its own margin, and in float
    otherwise; leaves are valued float-exact. Nothing adds up across nodes.
- So 128 is safe for all inputs, and so would be any count. For cell DAGs the next limit is MAX_STATES. Why 128 and not
  more: the gain shrinks with size, because child_into updates every node at each interior position, while float
  evaluates only the route. The review measured x0.58 at 121 nodes. Past 128, measure first.
- Doc-comment erratum: the MAX_NODES comment says "each under 2^20 units of 2^-32"; read "each under 2^20, i.e. 2^52
  units of 2^-32". The text stays in the source on purpose. A test-only edit to compound.rs already changed the
  build's md5 (561f865e... with it; checked), and hybrid20-src must rebuild the installed binary.

- Self-games (process CPU, calibration fits included): ply 2 x0.69-0.72, ply 3 x0.65, ply 4 x0.34.
- Own calibration: depth 1 x0.65-1.12 (at most 1.3 s a process, mostly the fits); depth 2 x0.37.
- The review measured on one pinned thread with its cap-256 build, which takes the same path at these sizes: ply 2
  x0.37 at 81-93 nodes and x0.58 at 121; ply 4 x0.39-0.50.
- **Argmax at ply 2 is slower (x1.44).**
  - Why: compound's depth-2 path values every root move (Search::windowable is false for it). hybrid19 played these
    DAGs in float with hybrid13's root windows, which at argmax (window next_down(best)) cut most root moves.
  - Models of 64 nodes or fewer already play argmax at ply 2 this way in hybrid19. On an under-64 field (gd300, d2,
    lib40, DC, F-P; 3 round-robins, 60 games, identical games) the default fixed point costs x1.22 the CPU of
    CHESSAI_FX=direct, which is float with windows (identity/extra/).
  - Nothing queued plays argmax at ply 2. Experiments play T 0.1 and rate at T 0.02 (x0.41). Arenas read at T 0.02.
  - A candidate for the next review: root windows in compound's (and fastprobit's) depth-2 path at argmax.
