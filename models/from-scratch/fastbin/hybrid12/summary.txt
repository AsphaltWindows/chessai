- Changed, only in the values-only fixed-point search (OUTCOME = false in negamax_fx: flat LR, probit and NB under
  root_values, which tournaments, arenas, replay-games (self-games) and the log-odds policies, softmax and argmax,
  use), at a node one ply above the leaves with a cut window (beta < inf):
  1. Not in check and without a pawn of the side to move on its seventh rank: best_capture finds, from the bitboards,
     a legal normal capture with the smallest MVV-LVA key (captures_first's key). A pinned piece may capture only
     along its pin's line; the king only onto a square the enemy doesn't attack once the king has left its own. That
     capture is searched first. The legal moves are generated, and searched in captures_first's order without it,
     only if it doesn't cut off. A legal move exists, so the node is neither mate nor stalemate; the draw checks
     (insufficient material, 50 moves, repetition) run as before.
  2. Otherwise (in check, no normal capture, a pawn about to promote, only en passant): after the usual end-of-game
     and draw checks, one scan of the generated moves finds the move captures_first would put first (the first of
     the smallest keys); the partition and sort happen only if it doesn't cut off. Without captures and promotions
     that is the first generated move, and the rest follow in their order: hybrid11's order exactly.
- Unchanged: the outcome search (root_outcomes, expected_outcome: self-play's default wld-score policies and search
  labels), the compound path (trees and DAGs at plies 1-2), fastprobit (F-P at ply 2) and the float negamax (trees and
  DAGs past ply 2, MoE, FM). Plies 1 and 2 never reach the new code (no cut window one ply above the leaves).
  CHESSAI_STAGED=off gives hybrid11's code everywhere, for checks. CHESSAI_FX_CHECK=1 also asserts each staged move
  legal and of the smallest key among the legal normal captures.

METHOD (why nothing can change)
- root_values gives every root move a full window, so each root value is the exact minimax value of its subtree
  (draws by repetition depend on the path, which a node's own move order doesn't change), in any move order. The
  values are the same f64s: negations and maxima of the same leaf values and terminal constants.
- Only the order inside a depth-1 cut node changes, and only by moving one move to the front. When keys tie,
  best_capture or the scan may pick another first move than captures_first's unstable sort, so node counts can change
  (as hybrid10's did): in the unit test, 8,459,731 nodes with staging off and 8,452,746 on. The values can't.
- The staged move is legal: not in check, a capture by a piece other than the king is illegal only if the piece is
  pinned and leaves its line; the king's capture is checked against the enemy's attacks without the king. En passant
  (the capture with a horizontal-pin subtlety) and promotions are left to the full generator.
