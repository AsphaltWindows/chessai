DEVIATIONS / CHOICES WHERE THE SPEC IS SILENT OR INCONSISTENT
  1. Probit k: num_params = weights.len() + 1 = 853 (851 values + bias + the draw margin): one latent's posterior
     means, not class_num x width as 0.7 lists for "Logreg / probit". LR is class_num x width. CNBP and TAN are
     as 0.7 says.
     A leaf split is k = 2 x 853 + 2556 (the CNBP router) = 4262, so bar_mdl = 0.0106 at n 3M and 0.0055 at 6M.
     Section 3's "bar ~0.004 at n 3M" implies k ~ 1600 and is inconsistent with 0.7's own formula. --grow-mdl
     rescales.
  2. The n of the verdict is the split node's num_trained_total at the split plus the trial's examples, which is
     n_v at judgement. The delegator's own counter restarts at the split (0.3).
  3. The split node's restart (0.3) keeps closes, backoff_windows and eligible_after_close. Back-off after an undo
     (0.8; test g: 1, 2, 4) needs them. Nodes made by a split start at zero.
  4. Extra per-node state field prev_model_mean (the model's mean over the node's last closed window). The
     excess of every candidate at a scheduling point uses its own last closed window.
  5. BDD refused under grow. Phase 1 covers BDT; the spec also names BDD leaves. hybrid15 refuses split trials with
     path context and with --router-split-limit (bdd.rs checked), and the fields' DAGs have both. Grow trials there
     need new trial code (phase 2).
  6. The node line's warning is printed with every node line while nodes >= 55 (after starts, keeps and undos),
     not only at the crossing.
  7. The first credit comes at N = D (next_credit_at starts at D). This fits test d's numbers and section 3's
     "root split at ~5M".
  8. A leaf's window closes at the check before its next example, as today, so closes log N = 1000001 and so on.
  9. The made-on-trial exclusion cannot be reached in a BDT: the children see fewer than W examples within their
     parent's W-example trial. It is kept as a safeguard and tested white-box.

OBSERVATIONS FOR THE ANALYST
  - "Exactly today's undo" sets the leaf's num_trained_total to the trial's examples (1M), although at forget
    factor 1 the shadow keeps the leaf's whole posterior. A retry after an undo is judged at a smaller n, so its
    MDL bar is higher. Leaf 1's retry, three windows after the undo, would be judged at n 5M (bar 0.0066)
    instead of its lifetime 8.6M (bar 0.0040).
  - With a single eligible leaf, the excess ordering does not matter: an easy leaf (excess -0.24) got the credit.
    The trial decides.
  - The block se of a trial's gain is ~7x the per-example se (0.0022 vs 0.00032 at the root), partly because the
    gain grows over the trial.
