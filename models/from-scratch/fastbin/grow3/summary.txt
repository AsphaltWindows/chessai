  (1) Share guard: a router is judged once its children holding at least S of their lifetime examples (the per-node
      `life` counter) have each closed a window (num_trained_total >= W); no lifetime examples, no judging.
      --grow-router-guard-share S, default 0.9 under stall-h/stall-hg; 1.0 = grow2's every-child guard (refused
      with no rule, z or subtree, and outside [0, 1]). Recorded in the state's options only when not 1.
  (2) Null shadows (stall-hg): beside the real shadows (path or cluster), as many copies of the router and its
      table, made and run with them, each scored before training and trained alike on the examples of its part of
      a hash partition (position_hash mod m, the cluster warm-up's). Per example d = G_real - G_null, blocked with
      the same 20-block windows; split iff mean d > 2 block SE and > the MDL bar. Logged (--log-router-stats):
      "G real; G null; G diff [passes]". The null is always on under stall-hg (no way to run HG without it); its
      shadows and blocks are in the state. Subtree keeps grow2's real-G rule.
  (3) k = (m-1) d_R under stall-h/stall-hg: cluster (B-1) d = 5,112 at B 3 (was (B+1) d = 10,224); path (m-1) d
      unchanged. Equivalent to grow2 at --grow-mdl 0.5. Subtree keeps (B+1) d.
