FINDINGS FOR THE ANALYST.
  1. The children guard (a child has closed a window = num_trained_total >= W) holds every router in the smoke:
     each has a starved child (end of the smoke: the root's leaf 3 has 556k, 3.2% of its traffic; router 2's leaf 5
     63k, 0.7%; router 1's 626k-1.18M; router 6's leaf 12 92k). The root would open at ~31M (~v100 at 300k a
     cycle) and router 1 soon after; router 2 practically never. Arm H may fire later and less than 6.41 expects.
     A share reading (children holding 90% of the router's traffic have closed a window, as subtree's 0.9) would
     open it; not built.
  2. G declines monotonically while the cluster shadows run (both routers, from 0 to -0.007 and -0.019). Training
     and scoring follow the router's own path (same target, same update, counts, scored before training); the
     synthetic tests behave. Possibly NB shadows trained on a hard-EM cluster's subset; worth a look before
     reading HG's refusals as "no routing gain".

DEVIATIONS (conservative readings).
  - k per spec 0.7 with d = router + path-table parameters: cluster (B+1) d = 10,224, path (m-1) d. 6.40's "k ~2,500"
    is one copy; --grow-mdl 0.25 reproduces its bar (0.002 at 10M).
  - Children ready = every direct child has num_trained_total >= W (see finding 1).
  - stall-h's split: cluster = today's split_delegator; path = unsharing into forgotten clones. stall-hg/subtree
    split with their shadows as the routers (cluster: q on top, S_j copies).
  - No router credits: a router splits at its own close, bounded by --router-split-limit and router splits < leaf
    splits; no ranking across routers.
  - Path case: any non-member router with 2+ parents; members use cluster under auto and never split under path.
    An unsharing drops the copies' cells of sets no longer upstream (pathctx::check needs it; their routing moves
    slightly). After an unsharing, the parents' routers use subtree-error targets for it.
  - H and G use the pre-training route and the realised child losses; the stagnation statistic is the recorded
    router log-loss; leaves keep their own 20-block windows under subtree; shares by grow_n.
  - Post-split check pooled over the made nodes (with v itself in an unsharing); back-off on fresh nodes is nearly a
    no-op. A split router's grow statistics reset fully. Shadows are dropped and restart when their router's parents
    change, else they get the new cells. Q_v is of the router kind (Cnbp), hash warm-up for its first W/5.
  - life counts recorded (scored) examples; v keeps its life after an unsharing.
  - Rule names in refusal messages print as StallH/StallHg (Debug), not the flag spelling.
