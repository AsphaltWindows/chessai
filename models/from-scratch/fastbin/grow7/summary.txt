WHAT. Two Train-only flags (not model options, so the state header and read_state are unchanged), only with --start,
only for bdt and bdd, applied once after the state is read:
  --warm-reset            (needs --split-rule grow) a growing tree or DAG keeps its structure and growth state
                          (credits, closes, back-off, router chain and counts) and:
                          - closes its leaf growth trials in flight on their statistic so far (grow_verdict over the
                            finished blocks); a trial with fewer than 2 finished blocks has no SE and is KEPT;
                          - (DAGs) closes its router trials in flight under their own keep rule (router_verdict over
                            the finished windows); a trial without a finished window is KEPT;
                          - restarts every node's windows (forget_window, open_window, GrowStats::restart), drops the
                            snapshots, the routers' shadows and null shadows and the pending post-split checks; path
                            tables carry over.
  --start-leaf-forget F   (F > 0; under the grow rule only with --warm-reset) every live leaf forgotten once (LR
                          AdaGrad sums / F, probit variances x F, CNBP counts / F) with its path table; routers and
                          revision shadows untouched; refused with a trial in flight or with leaf_residual.
Each logs what it did (the counts of trials closed, kept and undone; nodes restarted; leaves forgotten).
