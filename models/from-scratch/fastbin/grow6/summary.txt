WHAT
  grow5 plus two things, in one binary:
  1. grow4's leaf growth rule inside bdd, the DAG code (grow1-5 refused --split-rule grow for --model bdd). A DAG with
     --path-context may now take --split-rule grow and every grow4/grow5 leaf option (credits, trials, --grow-gate
     age, --grow-priority snapshot, --grow-mdl, --grow-min-gain, --grow-shadow-forget). The leaf trial's shadow keeps
     the leaf's path table (forgotten as the shadow is), the snapshot keeps a frozen copy of it, and an undone trial
     gives the leaf the shadow's table back with the cells upstream now. The MDL k counts path-table parameters too
     (zero without cells, so a tree's k is unchanged).
  2. --router-split-rule encourage (needs --split-rule grow and --path-context), with:
       --router-credit D_r          one router split credit per D_r positions through the root (default 10,000,000;
                                    banked up to --grow-credit-cap, 2; 0 = no allowance, no router splits)
       --router-trial-windows K     the long trial's length, K windows of --split-threshold examples through the
                                    split's region (default 5)
       --router-trial-z z           undo only if the trial's G_real - G_null is below -z block-SE (default 2)
       --router-chain N             a router may split only while its chain is below N (default 1: a copy, or a
                                    router that split, splits no more)
       --log-router-stats           (grow2's flag) the router-split, leaf-trial and path-cell logs below
     The split: at the end of a training step (no example in flight), if a router closed a window this step and a
     credit is banked, the eligible router with the highest last-window G_real - G_null splits, ties to the higher H,
     then the lower index. Eligible: a router, not removed, no leaf trial at it, outside every split on trial (its
     router, copies, their children and parents), chain below --router-chain, its own windows closed at least
     --grow-age-windows (3) times, out of back-off, children ready by the 90% share guard. No bar. The kind is 6.36's:
     by arrival path where the router has several parents, else by cluster; the copies are its shadows where they
     fit (grow2's cluster_split / unshare), else forgotten copies.
     The long trial: the router's NULL shadows (copies of the unsplit router over a hash partition of the split's
     shape, stall-hg's; carried over from before the split, fresh copies if it had none) run on as the unsplit
     alternative, each trained on its part of the region's examples. Per scored example: d = the loss of the
     split's pick minus the loss of the null shadow's pick among the old children, both before training; G_real -
     G_null = -mean(d), so both arms lag alike (each copy and each null shadow learns from a part of the examples:
     6.46 (iii) 2's drift). Beside them a copy of the router as it was trains on every example: the router an undo
     restores, and the plain G_real (split against the unsplit router, logged only). After K windows: undone iff
     G_real - G_null < -z block-SE; otherwise kept, whatever its gain.
     The undo: a cluster split's central router routes among its old children again, an unsharing's parents point
     to the router again; the copies go (removed at the next step); a cluster split's sibling set leaves the
     registry with its cells (later sets renumbered in every table); the router takes the unsplit router and its
     table, conformed to the cells upstream, leaves the chain, and backs off (age again, then 2x the back-off).
  Source: fastbin/grow6-src (a copy of grow5-src; diff grow6.patch, 4 files: delegation.rs, bdd.rs, pathctx.rs,
  main.rs). rs/ untouched. Model files keep their format (the new state is in state files only, and only under the
  new options), so hybrid17 loads and plays grow6's DAGs.
  Binary: cand/chessai-grow6, installed with cp -n as models/from-scratch/bin/chessai-grow6 (md5 below). Built like
  grow5 (cargo release); the build embeds its paths, so identity is tested on behaviour.

AGAINST 6.80 (what the build had to decide or change)
  1. The trial's paired statistic is G_real - G_null against the null shadows, not the split against the unsplit
     router. 6.80: "the shadow pair runs K = 5 own windows; undone only if its paired statistic is below -2
     block-SE". After the split the real shadows are the copies, so the pair is the split and the null shadows (the
     hash partition of the same shape, stall-hg's), which run on as the unsplit alternative. A router trained on
     every example as the only comparator would make every split look worse by lag alone (each copy and each null
     shadow learns from a part of the examples: 6.46 (iii) 2, 6.54), so the undo would prune by lag. The plain
     G_real against the unsplit router is still computed (that router is what an undo restores) and logged per
     window and at the verdict, for the read; it decides nothing.
  2. The trial's length: K windows of --split-threshold examples through the split's region (the router and its
     copies together), K x W = 5M at the recipe's W 1M. After an unsharing the router alone sees only its own
     arrival path, so "own windows" of the router alone would lengthen the trial by the number of paths.
  3. The allowance's bank: router credits are banked up to --grow-credit-cap (2), a bank of their own beside the
     leaves'; an undone router split refunds nothing (the allowance stays one split per D_r, whatever is pruned).
  4. The chain: --router-chain 1 counts the router that split and its copies alike (6.80: "a copy can't
     router-split"), so a router that split once, kept, splits no more; an undo puts the router's chain back.
  5. After an undo the router backs off: --grow-age-windows own closes again, then a back-off that doubles with
     each undo (1, 2, 4 ... closes), as the leaves' trials do. 6.80 has no rule for re-splitting a pruned router.
  6. When: a router split happens at the end of a training step (no example in flight), at a step in which some
     router closed a window, if a credit is banked. 6.80 gives the rate, not the moment.
  7. Eligibility adds: no leaf trial at the router (a leaf trial's delegator is not a router yet and its closes do
     not count as router closes), and nothing inside a split on trial (its router, copies, their children and
     parents), so trials never overlap.
  8. The MDL bar's k for a leaf split counts the path-table parameters too (cells x values; about 4 per cell for
     probit against ~800 per probit leaf, so ~1% of k), zero without cells: the tree's bar and the allowance-off
     DAG's are unchanged.
  9. Collapsed routing: a path split whose copies, null shadows and unsplit router always pick the same child gives
     a statistic of exactly 0 (se 0); the bar is then 0 rather than -z x 0, and a split that is not behind is kept
     (the xor3 smoke kept such splits at N 16970 and 17059). 6.80 prunes only on harm, so this follows it.
  10. Options: --router-split-rule encourage needs --split-rule grow and --path-context, and refuses
     --router-split-limit (the allowance replaces it), --router-split-z and --grow-subtree-share; the four new
     flags are refused without encourage. A growing DAG without encourage must say --router-split-z inf.
  11. No binary-level resume test (P1 cannot give an empty round); the unit test resumes every 997 steps instead.
