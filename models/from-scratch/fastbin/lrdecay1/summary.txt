chessai-lrdecay1 (2026-11-12 ~05:15; analyst 6.64 (b) and its note; built by the coordinator's build fork)
=====================================================================================================

WHAT
  grow5 plus one training option, --lr-sum-memory N: decaying AdaGrad sums for logistic regression. Each example of
  the model's own multiplies every AdaGrad sum by (1 - 1/N), then adds its squared gradients:
      S <- S (1 - 1/N) + g^2      (the sum-memory form, not an RMSProp mean, as the analyst asked)
  A gradient's weight in the sums falls by e after about N examples, so the steps stop shrinking for good.
  - Without the option, or with N = 0: AdaGrad as before, byte for byte.
  - For `train` of --model lr (F-L) and of --model bdt with --leaf lr and no --leaf-residual (T-L-*).
  - Refused elsewhere (exit 1): N = 1, other models (cnbp, probit, bdd, ...), --leaf probit, --leaf-residual, and
    self-play.
  Source: fastbin/lrdecay-src, a copy of grow5-src (rs/ untouched). Its diff is lrdecay1.patch, 7 files:
  - logreg.rs: the memory, the age and the lazy step (see DESIGN), settle, the state I/O, and 5 tests.
  - delegation.rs: DelegationParams.lr_sum_memory, which the root leaf gets. Debug prints it only when set, so state
    files without it read as before and a resumed run must pass the same N. Classifier 4 in state files is "LR with
    a memory": the LR state, then the memory and the age.
  - classifier.rs: the flat Logreg's refresh() settles the sums.
  - main.rs: the option, its checks, and the flat model gets the memory after --start.
  - bdt.rs: 1 test plus 4 memory variants in the state round-trip test.
  - bdd.rs, compound.rs: the new field in their test parameter literals.
  Untouched: probit, CNBP, routers (only the root LEAF gets the memory, LR routers never do), play code, model-file
  formats.
  Binary: cand/chessai-lrdecay1, md5 cab29ea7292160ce1fdd4c2fd32a8d64, built like grow5 (nice 19 cargo build
  --release -j 2). Installed with cp -n as models/from-scratch/bin/chessai-lrdecay1.

DESIGN (Delta = the node's own examples, as the analyst's note asks)
  Every Logreg (the flat model, each leaf, each shadow) keeps its own `age`: the examples it has stepped on since its
  sums were last settled. Each step is one example, including a --leaf-share step of a sibling.
  - Held sums: the stored sum is U = S (1 - 1/N)^(-age). The decayed step adds g^2 to S and moves the weight by
    rate g / sqrt(S); in held terms that is U += c g^2 and rate sqrt(c) g / sqrt(U), with c = (1 - 1/N)^(-age).
  - So it is AdaGrad's own step (the AVX2 kernel, unchanged) on the gradient times sqrt(c): the example's weight and
    its L2 term are both scaled by exp(age kappa / 2), with kappa = -ln(1 - 1/N), computed with ln_1p.
  - One exp per example. Only the example's own weights are touched. The decay of every other sum is the growth of
    c, so no per-weight stamps are needed.
  - It is the per-weight stamp scheme exactly (S_i <- S_i (1 - 1/N)^Delta_i + g^2, with Delta_i in the model's own
    examples), up to rounding.
  - settle(): multiplies U by (1 - 1/N)^age and sets age 0, keeping the values up to rounding. It runs:
    - before an example would take age kappa above 32 (U stays within e^32 of S);
    - in the flat model's refresh(), before each save;
    - when the memory changes.
  - Copies carry the memory and the age. A split's new leaves, the growth trial's shadow, the revision shadow and
    the grow4 snapshot are clones of the leaf: same N, same age, held sums divided by the forget factor. Each then
    counts its own examples.
  - blank() (reset leaves) keeps the memory at age 0. forget() divides U, i.e. divides S.
  - Model files keep their format: the settled sums, without N or age, so hybrid16 loads them (checked, same scores).
    - Flat F-L resumes from its model file. The file is written settled at age 0, and --lr-sum-memory is passed
      again, so the resume is exact.
    - Trees resume from state files, where classifier 4 keeps the held sums and the age exactly.

OFFLINE SWEEP (sweep.py -> sweep.txt, sweep/results.json)
  Setup:
  - F-L, one pass, seed 7101: AdaGrad, and N = 1M, 2M, 4M, 8M.
  - Prequential by round: round r is scored by the model trained on rounds 0..r-1, as a league line plays the next
    cycle. Raw, and with a calibration fitted on each round (in sample, the same for every arm), to separate ranking
    from calibration.
  - Gain = AdaGrad's loss minus N's loss, in nats per position, paired by round. SE is over rounds, which is
    optimistic under autocorrelation.
  Streams:

    softmax T 0.1). 1728 games a round (about one league cycle of an F-L line, ~210k positions), 150 rounds, 31.5M

  - p2drift: offline-ply/drift, P2x's 54 rounds then P2's 54 (an abrupt change of field).
  - The pools' rounds are merged per round for eval-loss, as calib.py did (scratchpad, RAM).

  READING
  - Every N loses to AdaGrad on every stream, raw and calibrated, by more the shorter the memory: roughly in
    proportion to 1/N, halving with each doubling.
  - The losses grow along the stream: sp thirds at 1M are -0.0038, -0.0070, -0.0076; at 8M -0.0004, -0.0010,
    -0.0015.
  - The drifting stream behaves like the stationary pool. Only rounds 1-3 of sp gain at all, by 0.0001-0.0004 (rounds
    4-9 already lose 0.0003-0.0028).
  - After the abrupt change (p2drift rounds 54-63) the losses shrink at every N, 4M and 8M by about half (8M: -0.0017
    before, -0.0009 and -0.0008 right after), but never turn into gains. Then they grow again.
  - The calibrated losses are 2/3 to 4/5 of the raw ones (at 1M: sp 0.66, p2drift 0.76, p1 0.81). So most of the cost
    is ranking (noisier weights), not only overconfidence.
  - Mechanism, as far as the numbers go: at the memories tested, the larger steps add more weight noise than the
    faster tracking removes. Each coordinate's memory is N of the MODEL's examples, as specified, so a feature value
    seen in a fraction f of positions averages over only about f N of its own updates against f t under AdaGrad. The
    rare piece-square values are the noisiest.
  - Against 6.64:
    - Predicted prequential gain on drift: 0.003-0.008 at 1-2M and 0.001-0.003 at 8M. Measured on sp: -0.0062 and
      -0.0037 at 1-2M, -0.0010 at 8M.
    - Predicted stationary held-out cost: 0.000-0.003 at 8M and 0.003-0.008 at 1M. Measured with the fitted
      calibration: 0.0015 at 8M (inside) and 0.0063 at 1M (inside). Measured raw: 0.0074 and 0.0259 (outside).
  RULE (the analyst's note): the largest N whose drifting-stream prequential gain is within 10% of the best N's. No N
  gains (the best is 8M at -0.0010), so the rule picks nothing.
  Caveat: offline, the stream does not respond to the learner. A league line's own play would drift with its
  learning (on-policy feedback), which no offline stream has, and the league's field (trees, frozen references) may
  drift faster than AdaGrad F-L self-play does.
