chessai-grow5 (2026-11-12 ~04:15; analyst theory-2 6.62 (a); built by the coordinator's build fork)
==================================================================================================

WHAT
  grow4 plus one training option, --grow-shadow-forget {split|none}. The default is split. It applies to BDT with
  --split-rule grow only; anything else refuses it, as with grow4's leaf options.
  - split (grow1-4's behaviour): a growth trial's shadow, the leaf without the split, starts as a copy of the leaf
    forgotten by --forget-factor, just like the split's new leaves.
  - none: the shadow starts as the leaf itself, unforgotten. The new leaves still forget.
  Source: fastbin/grow5-src, a copy of grow4-src. Its diff is grow5.patch: 4 files, of which only delegation.rs and
  main.rs change behaviour.
  - delegation.rs: the GrowShadowForget enum and the GrowParams field. split_leaf's growth branch skips
    shadow.forget() under none. GrowParams' Debug prints shadow_forget only when it is none, so state files written
    by grow1-4, or by grow5 without the option, read byte for byte as before.
  - main.rs: the option, plus grow4_given() and its refusal message.
  - logreg.rs: a squares() accessor for the tests only (#[cfg(test)]).
  - bdt.rs: 3 tests.
  Untouched: the children's forget and split noise, the non-growth split trial's shadow, the revision shadow,
  Graph::forget, BDD, routers and all play code. Model files are unchanged: the option is not saved in them, and
  hybrid16 loads and scores grow5's models (below).
  Binary: cand/chessai-grow5, md5 d75f0f7a8312ea34ac3245a7ea9a45c3. It was built like grow4 (cargo release) and is
  installed with cp -n as models/from-scratch/bin/chessai-grow5. A build of unchanged grow4 source from this copy
  also differs from grow4's md5, because of the embedded build paths, so identity is tested on behaviour.

UNDO, REFUND AND SNAPSHOTS
  On an undone trial, end_trial sets node.classifier = trial.shadow. The node then trains on the verdict step's
  example like any leaf.
  - split (today): the restored leaf is the forgotten shadow. It is the leaf as it was at the split with its AdaGrad
    sums divided by 10 (a probit shadow's variances widened instead), trained on every example of the trial, then on
    the verdict step's example. Every trial, kept or undone, therefore leaves the leaf boosted.
  - none: the restored leaf is the leaf as it would have continued: its own unforgotten sums plus everything the
    shadow accumulated during the trial, then the verdict step's example.
  The refund (credit +1 up to the cap, back-off doubled) and the snapshots are the same in both modes. grow_restart
  drops the leaf's snapshot at the trial's start and grow_undone at the undo, and the next window close freezes a
  new one.

TESTS
  1. cargo test (unit-tests.log): 233 lib and 1 main test pass. The new tests are:
     - grow5_the_shadow_keeps_the_leafs_sums_under_none: forget 10, split_leaf called on a trained LR leaf, both
       modes. The new leaves' sums are leaf/10 and their state equals the forgotten clone, in both modes. The shadow
       is leaf/10 under split and exactly the leaf (state bytes) under none.
     - grow5_an_undone_trial_leaves_the_carried_shadow: forget 10, W 200, trial z inf so every trial is undone, two
       undos per mode, followed example by example. A leaf splits at the start of its step, so the shadow is checked
       first, as the pre-step leaf (forgotten under split) trained on that step's example. At the verdict the
       pre-step shadow equals a replay of that start trained on every trial example, and the restored leaf equals
       the replay trained on the verdict's example. The restored sums are >= the leaf's sums at the split everywhere
       under none; under split some are below half of them, tracing back to the tenth.
     - grow5_shadow_forget_is_recorded_only_when_set: the default's Debug lacks the field; none ends
       "..., warn_nodes: 55, shadow_forget: None }".
