WHAT CHANGED (fastbin/hybrid15-src = hybrid14-src plus hybrid15.patch: src/compound.rs and src/delegation.rs)
- delegation.rs: Graph::descend_from(node, data, marks), the float descent from a node with its path marks (the
  fixed-point path's fallback when a router's margin is inside its bound).
- compound.rs:
  - Cells: every (node, sorted marks) a path reaches from the root (a DFS over router children; at most 4,096
    states, else the DAG plays in float as on hybrid14), each with the cell terms its path context adds:
    - LR routers: the cells' log-odds offsets in fixed point (round(offset x SCALE)); CNBP routers: ln of the cells'
      class factors (finite and positive only, else float). Each router state's decision margin is recomputed from
      the node's bound plus the offsets' rounding and evaluation error.
    - LR leaves: the logit difference the cells add (with its absolute value and error); probit leaves: the cells'
      latent mean and variance (mu, s2).
  - route_cells: the incremental fixed-point sums plus the state's offsets; a router decides in fixed point when the
    top class clears the state's margin (plus a product check on the raw top), else descends in float
    (descend_from) and looks the state up. CHESSAI_FX_CHECK=1 asserts every decision against node_predict_path.
  - Leaf estimates with bounds as hybrid5/hybrid9's, shifted by the state's terms (probit: mean + mu, var + s2 with
    their extra error), so best() values in float only the replies that can be a move's minimum, as before; exact
    values from node_predict_path (the float model's path code).
  - The cell-less path is numerically unchanged (estimate refactored through estimate_probit with zero shifts).
- New unit test dags_with_cells_value_exactly: ctx BDDs with LR and probit leaves trained until they have 2+ sibling
  sets (router-split z 0), every searched position's values bit-equal to the float search. 196 tests pass
  (unit-tests.log); the compound tests pass with CHESSAI_FX_CHECK=1 too.

NOT VERIFIED / LIMITS
- Ply 3 and deeper play the float path (identical at depth 3 in play_id15). Outcome search not compared on DAGs.
- DAGs over 64 nodes (hybrid5's cap) or with more than 4,096 route states play in float: exact, but at hybrid14's
  speed. The state count of a DAG grows with its sibling sets; the final cell DAGs (D-P z0, D-L z0, D-P z30#2) have 25 nodes and 607, 607 and 445 route states (probe:
  hybrid15/probe-src, a test-only copy). At split limit 4 and router limit 4 they are full size (4 splits, 4 sibling sets
  of 3), so the global league's DAGs stay inside the caps; nodes = 1 + 3 x (splits + router splits), so the 64-node
  cap binds past 21 splits in all.
