ARENA OWN-GAMES CACHE: leagues/arena.py SHARES OWN CALIBRATION GAMES ACROSS ARENAS (2026-11-11 ~01:10 to ~01:35, the
hybrid10 implementation agent, from the 11-11 ~00:50 performance review's item 2)

RESULT
- leagues/arena.py (backup: leagues/arena.py.before-owncache, md5 b5b842d3...; new md5 6bcd4cef...). Replaced at
  ~01:30 by a rename of a verified copy (leagues/.arena-owncache.py) after checking that arena.py was still the
  backup's bytes. No arena.py process ran then. Processes that imported the old module (the dashboard) keep it until
  they restart; nothing they call changed.
- What changed:
  - make_own takes an entry's own games from leagues/arenas/.owncal-cache/<key>/ when they are cached. It copies them
    with cp --reflink=auto and checks each copy's size and md5 against the entry's key.json. Games it makes go into
    the cache, read-only, with key.json.
  - own_cache_key: sha256 over the `arena` command with the paths left out (own_args, own_play), the md5 of the
    arena's model copy, the md5 of the held-out games (None without) and the md5 of the owncal binary. The game
    files hold no path (checked: no "/home", "models/" or "owncal" in them).
  - A store onto a key already cached (two runs at once) compares the bytes. A difference logs a WARNING and drops the
    cache entry.
  - cache_trim keeps the cache to 20 GB (apparent sizes; oldest by last use first) and removes .tmp- leftovers older
    than a day.
  - prepare() logs "N from the shared cache, M made (X CPU s)". drop_own_games is unchanged; its message now says
    the next run takes the games from the cache.
  - Config key "owncal_cache" (default true; arenas made before have none and use the cache). false gives the old
    behaviour exactly. keep_own_games keeps its meaning: false still removes the arena's own copy when a run ends.
  - CHESSAI_OWNCAL_CACHE moves the cache, for tests.
- The cache was seeded with the test's 31 verified entries: ply3-cold, cold-final-probit-widen-p1b, nb-settings and
  cal2-routing; 362 MB apparent size, reflinked. key.json "arena" names the original arena and "seeded" says how
  they were made.
- leagues/ARENA.md: the Calibration section, the defaults and the owncal/ files.

EXACTNESS (cachetest.py, remake.py, cachetest2.py; logs cachetest.log, cachetest2.log; 71 checks, 0 failures)

  probit, fm; hybrid8 and hybrid9 own games), diag/cold-final-probit-widen-p1b (probit, bdt with probit leaves),
  pairs/nb-settings (cnbp; hybrid4gfx and hybrid9) and diag/cal2-routing (lr and bdt with LR leaves, calibrated at

  2. drop_own_games, then the same step from the cache: 31 of 31 entries restored. The restored games are

     hybrid8 and hybrid9.
- Other paths (cachetest2.py):

  - a damaged entry (a byte flipped) or a missing one is refused and leaves no directory;

  - a trim below the cache size removes the oldest entries and a stale .tmp- dir;
  - with owncal_cache false the games are made and the cache isn't touched.
- `arena.py status` prints byte-identical output with the old and the new arena.py (ply3-cold).
