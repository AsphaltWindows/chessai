Topic 4c (2026-11-07/08 night): do unstarved leaves let trees grow? Leaf sharing at three to four times the leaves.

Verdict: sharing makes size nearly free in accuracy, but not useful. No line.
- Without sharing, trees with 25-33 leaves are clearly worse than with 7-11.
- With each family's best share, 27-35 leaves are level with 7-11 (differences 0.0003-0.0011, not significant).
- The share's training cost grows with the number of leaves.

Setup (size.sh; size2.sh -> size.out, parts.csv):
- offline-c15 as ../leshare/dose.sh, on chessai-sparsefit: train on round-robins 80-83, 4 rounds, score on round 84's
  10 parts, calibrated.
- Bigger trees: split threshold 30k and split limit 20, against 100k and 5. At 100k the trees made 3-5 splits, and
  le-s03 hit the limit.
- Each family is run with and without its best share from the dose sweep: Nc 0.1, Ne 0.3, Le 0.3.
- Run notes:
  - A restart of the script truncated the first run's output, and a duplicate restart was stopped mid-way.
  - size2.sh retrained the interrupted tree and evaluated all six.
  - Training is deterministic, and the retrained trees reproduce the first run's.

Reading:
- The size penalty without sharing is starvation: 29-33 leaves at about 75k positions each.
- Sharing removes it, but the extra leaves find nothing the 7-11 leaves didn't. On 2.4M positions, more
  conditioning contexts carry no more information for LR leaves; that is either data per leaf or what the routers
  split on.
- The league's lineages are in another regime. At a 5M threshold every leaf sees millions of positions, and the
  limit of 5 splits (11 leaves) binds eventually. Whether share lineages could use a higher limit there is open; this
  benchmark can't say.

Idea, not run:
- T-L-Nc-share-c with --split-limit 20, one factor from T-L-Nc-share-c, would test the league regime directly.
- Offline, size is free with sharing, so it is unlikely to be broken. Its cost grows with leaves: sharing makes
  every leaf step on every example.
- A cheaper share that scales would sample k other leaves per example, at weight share x (L-1)/k.

CPU: 6 trainings, 230 user-s (plus about 40 retrained), and evaluations. About 6 CPU-min.
