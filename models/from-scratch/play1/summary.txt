The Play page: a game against a library model at a chosen ply (2026-11-12 ~17:05-17:50, a worker fork)
==========================================================================================================

The user: "I'd like another option in the menu that leads to a chess board where I can play against a game against a
chosen model in the library at a chosen ply. Maybe you can grab lichess open source library for the UI if you think it
makes things easier and it's possible to integrate with it."

HOW TO USE
----------
http://127.0.0.1:8766/play/  (top bar: Play; Home: the Play tile)
1. Choose the opponent. The list shows the fully plateaued models and the holdouts by default (Show: all models for
   the rest). Narrow it by Type (F, T, D), Kind, Trained at (ply 1-4, other) or Find. Sort it by ladder Elo (the
   default), type, training ply or experiment. The chosen model's run, regime, end and ladder Elo show below.
2. Choose the ply, 1 to 4; it starts at the model's training ply. Choose your colour: White, Black or Random. Choose
   how it plays: "its best move" (the log-odds argmax, the default) or "as in the arenas" (log-odds softmax T 0.02,
   seeded).
3. Press New game. Move by dragging, by clicking the two squares, or by typing SAN (Nf3) or UCI (g1f3). A promotion
   asks for the piece; Cancel or Escape takes the move back. The buttons: Take back (to before your last move;
   counted), Flip board, Resign.
4. The page shows:
   - the move list, with the engine's time for each of the model's moves;
   - the calibration (the experiment's own parameters);
   - the model's White/draw/Black probabilities before and after its move;
   - its search's value of its move and its four best moves.
5. A finished game (mate, draw, resignation) is saved as leagues/play-games/<date>-<time>-<id>v<V>-p<ply>-<colour>.pgn
   and listed on the page. Its tags: LibraryId, Experiment, Line, Version, Model, Archetype, Kind, TrainPly, Ply,
   Policy, Seed, Takebacks, Engine, ModelFile, ModelMd5, Calibration, Started, Termination, PlyCount. A game left by
   New game is not saved.

LIMITATIONS
-----------
- A possible repetition edge: chess.js sets an en-passant square whenever an enemy pawn is beside the pushed pawn,
  even when that pawn is pinned; the engine counts only legal en-passant squares. So a threefold count could differ
  by one. chess.js decides on the page: if the engine thinks the game is over and chess.js doesn't, the page says
  so and play goes on. Never seen in the checks.
- In argmax play, ties between equally valued moves are broken by the game's seed, as in the tournaments, so two
  games from the same moves can differ.
- A model whose own calibration games aren't in the arenas' cache makes them on its first New game: seconds (3 s
  for the grow6 DAG).
- The list is leagues/library/index.csv as built (17:34:04, 481 rows, 284 shown by default). Newer runs appear
  only after leagues/library/build_index.py.
- No clocks, premoves, draw offers, or starting from a position or a PGN.
- One engine request at a time. Two tabs playing at once queue each other; each request takes milliseconds.
- The engine process stays up, idle, after the first game until the dashboard stops: nice 19, 1 thread, ~8 MB
  plus its models.
- chessground is GPL-3.0. That is fine for this local page; if the page is ever distributed, the GPL's terms apply.
