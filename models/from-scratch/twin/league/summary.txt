WHAT THE OWN STEP TRAINS ON (cycles/champ/cycle.py: own_steps, games_of, own_opponents, own_step)
  Games: every game of the cycle's 5 round-robins (tournaments 5(c-1)..5c-1) between one of the lineage's current
    entries (vN-1, vN-2; not the champion's entry) and any field player but the references (random, random-1..3,
    ref-d4mix43-1). Both colours, each game once; the two current entries' games against each other count too.
  Positions: all of each game's positions, labelled by the game's result, as in self-play.
  Order: the game directories are linked as {t:03d}~{white}~{black}; train reads them in name order and shuffles
    with seed 21, round 0 (derive_seed(21, 0, MAX)). The shuffle depends only on the number of positions.
    The names are those of the time: the 45->46 deploy renamed the MoE lineages (MoE-c -> M-L4-L-c, ...). Linked
    under today's names, the retrained step differs; linked under the names of the time, it is identical.
    check_cycle.py gets the old names by matching each own-games set's calibration with the tournament log's.
  Check: plan_own.py finds every kept cycle's games and plies equal to cycles.csv's own_games and own_positions
    (all 37 kept steps, plan-T-L-Ne-share-c.txt).

REPLAY
  Seeds: the tournament seeds every game derive_seed(1, t, g), g its pairing index in the field's order, so one
    lineage's games replay alone (replay-games).
  Field order: games.csv appends each round-robin in pairing order, which gives the field's order. It is checked
    for every kept cycle (order ok).
  Calibration: entries are calibrated on their own games (600 games, d1, seed 5).
    - Sets still in cycles/champ/owncal or the seed directory: copied.
    - Deleted sets (most of them): remade by the command recorded in KEY.arena. All 147 remade sets matched the
      recorded result.
    - At load, every calibration equals one the cycle's tournament logged, by value: 136 for cycle 45, 125 for
      cycle 43.
  Binary: the cycle's tournament binary, from tournament.log.
  Results:
    cycle 45 (v446): 4050 of 4050 games equal games.csv (result, plies); 360574 positions = own_positions.
    cycle 43 (v433): 3710 of 3710 games equal; 329298 positions = own_positions.
