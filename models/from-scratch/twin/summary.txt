Twin pilot (2026-11-08/09): T-L-Ne-share-c vs a flat LR "twin" trained on exactly its self-play games.
Question: on the same data, does the routed tree beat a flat model?
Answer for this tree: yes, by about 290 Elo at v428/v452. The flat twin is an ordinary flat LR (about F-L-c at the
same version), so the tree's self-play games are not a better curriculum.
Still open: is the edge the model, or the own steps' league data (9.9% of the tree's positions, which this twin
lacks)? The full-data twin settles it; see league/summary.txt.

METHOD
  Games: each self-play round r (v_r vs v_{r-1}, 1000 games) replayed with chessai-lrshare arena from the tree's own
    model files: bdt{r} vs bdt{r-1}, --use-probs --games 1000 --seed derive_seed(21, r, 0) (seed.py). The other
    defaults equal self-play's. Rounds 0-1 (bdt0 is never saved) come from a scratch self-play with the lineage's
    flags; its bdt1/bdt2 are byte-identical to the run's.
    All 416 rounds matched the log exactly (A's +W -L =D and plies; logs/progress.txt, no FAIL).
  Twin: F-L-c's recipe, chessai-cyc5 train --model lr --use-probs (defaults, rate 0.05), from scratch, round by
    round in the tree's order. Streamed (stream.py): generate, check, train, delete.
    - Round r is trained alone with --start and seed 21^sm(r)^sm(0). This is byte-identical to one train --seed 21
      over all rounds (checked on rounds 0-1).
    - train differs from self-play's training only in the within-round shuffle. On round 0, train's weights
      correlate 0.947 with self-play's, about the same as two train seeds do with each other (0.956).
    - LR training is byte-identical in chessai-cyc5 and chessai-lrshare.
  Own steps: 36 kept versions (28 52 67 ... 446) trained on league-pool games that aren't kept, so the twin stays
    unchanged at them. They are 9.9% of the tree's training positions (11.5M of 116.2M; ownshare2.py), and the
    twin lacks them.
    Their value is unknown: a league position is not worth a self-play one. The 36 kept own steps gained a median
    69 Elo over the pre-step model in their 100-game gates (quartiles 37 to 99; kept ones only, so biased up).
    The full-data twin (league/summary.txt) is the test.
  Sanity: through round 27 the tree was still one leaf (leaves: v28 1, v122 5, v452 11). Its games through round 27
    are F-L-c's (identical W/L/D), and twin v28's weights correlate 0.967 with F-L-c v28's, i.e. shuffle noise.
  Kept: twin models at the tree's 45 league versions, including champion v428 and current v452 (lr/).
  Matches (compare.sh, control.sh): chessai-sparsefit arena, depth 2 both sides, log-odds-softmax T0.1,
    --use-probs, seed 11, 2000 games. Each side is calibrated on its own games, made as the driver makes them
    (600 games, d1, seed 5, from heldout-lr-d2-s555). These are identical to the driver's for tree v428, tree v452
    and F-L-c v4100 (determinism.txt).

NOT SEPARATED
  The test can't tell routing/specialisation apart from the leaves' younger AdaGrad sums (bigger steps; see
  leafcmp).
  The near-flat D-L-Nc-c twin is the control: its full tree equals its leaf 3 in play. If it too beats its twin by
  a lot, the edge is youth rather than routing.
  A one-factor follow-up on the same games: a flat twin whose AdaGrad sums are forgotten, or with a larger rate.
