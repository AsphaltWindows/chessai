- BDD minus BDT, held out, one seed: log-loss -0.0018 [-0.0043, 0.0006], winner log-loss 0.0001 [-0.0017, 0.0019]
  (10 test parts, paired t, 9 df). Whole test set with a fitted calibration: BDD 0.6229 / 0.3321, BDT 0.6242 / 0.3316.
- Splits (split threshold 100k, split limit 5, 2.3M positions): the BDD made all 5 splits, 3 leaf splits and 2
  delegator splits; the BDT made 3 leaf splits. Both end with 7 leaves.
- The two delegator splits form one chain: the root became a router over sub-routers 4, 5, 6 (copies of its old
  router, all routing to its old children 1, 2, 3), and sub-router 4 split again into 13, 14, 15. So it is the Nov 5
  "chain of routers among the same children" shape. It is not degenerate in what it does: the sub-routers route
  differently on the test set (routing-de.txt):
  - root: 74% to 4, 4% to 5, 22% to 6; 4: 90% to 13, 4% to 14, 6% to 15;
  - 6 sends 99.9% to child 3; 13 spreads 30/38/33 over 1, 2, 3; 5 sends 69% to child 1; 15 sends 44% to child 3.
- In the league (5M split threshold) the delegator splits will take part of the 5-split budget, so D-L-Ne-c may end
  with fewer leaves than T-L-Ne-c's 11. That is part of the design, not a change of recipe.
