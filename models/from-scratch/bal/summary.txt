What --error-target balanced does (models/from-scratch/balsrc = sparsefit-src + bal.patch; bin/chessai-bal, md5
d6145e866f471cb5cda08531b597cd4c):
- The target is Best's one-hot on the best child. The router step is weighted by w = 1/(K pbar_best), clipped to
  [0.1, 10]: K the branch factor, pbar the delegator's running frequency of each child being best (an EMA over
  BALANCE_HORIZON = 1M examples through that delegator, starting uniform; reset when the node splits). LR routers
  take a weighted AdaGrad step (as leaf sharing does), CNBP routers train_weighted. The router then learns about
  p(k|x)/pbar(k), so its plain argmax routes by lift.
- Only error-routed delegators (train_router with --route error) are weighted; switch error routers and BDD
  comparisons treat balanced as Best. Play and model files are unchanged.
- pbar is a tail of the state file, written only with balanced, so other states are byte-identical to before. A
  state without the tail (older, or trained with Best) resumes balanced with uniform frequencies; a state's
  options may differ in Best/Balanced only (the error target only changes how routers learn from then on).
- Unit tests (139 pass, 4 new): balanced routes a weak signal by lift where Best piles onto the majority (LR and
  CNBP routers); the frequencies follow the EMA from uniform and the weight clips at 10; states round trip with the
  frequencies and mix with Best both ways while other options must still match; non-LR/CNBP routers are refused.
