MODEL OPTIMIZATION STUDY (2026-11-09, 16:28-17:30; a fork of the main session)
Nothing was deployed or switched. The only files written outside models/from-scratch/modelopt/ are two binaries:
models/from-scratch/bin/chessai-fxlr (v1) and models/from-scratch/bin/chessai-fxlr2 (v2). rs/, the live league and
leagues/ were only read.

Question: where do the base models spend CPU in play (d0 self-play, d1, d2) and in training, what can be cut, and does
a fixed-point incremental evaluation keep games repeatable?

Versions. Every result below says which binary it comes from:
- hybrid4: the current binary, the baseline (a copy is modelopt/bin/chessai-hybrid4, md5 1e050a863fb44b060592ed5b935bc2df).
- v1: models/from-scratch/bin/chessai-fxlr, md5 0c7159d054146efdf88c0ead0df3eeb4. The first fixed-point version.
- v2: models/from-scratch/bin/chessai-fxlr2, md5 93e370d678d9a5c4db5b6603bf0f0326. v1 with a cheaper incremental
  update. THIS IS THE ONE TO ADOPT: faster than v1 and it passed every test v1 did, plus more.
- modelopt/bin/ holds the same two binaries under the same names.

0. HEADLINES
=====================================================================================================================
- Profiles of hybrid4. Flat models (LR, probit, NB) at d2: summing the model is ~47% of a game, and the calibrated
  value's softmax, exp and ln are another ~26%. Trees: routing 46-65% plus the leaf 18-26%, so the model is 72-83% of
  a game. Mixtures: 69-85%. FM: 90%.
- Built: fixed-point incremental sums for LR, probit and NB, and direct log-odds values for every calibrated model.
  v2's CPU against hybrid4, in the same runs (section 3):
  - LR: d2 -66% (3.0x), d1 -53%, d0 -51%, d0 self-play with its training -31%.
  - NB: d2 -67% (3.0x), d1 -49%.
  - Probit: d2 -32%, d0 self-play -10%. The normal CDF math stays.
  - Search-label training (train --label-depth 2): -69%, and the trained model is byte-identical.
  - Trees, DAGs, mixtures, FM and HT, which get direct values only: d2 -2 to -14%, mean -8%.
- v1 against v2, interleaved on the same machine load: v2 is 16% faster at LR d2, 5% at probit d0 self-play and 1.5%
  at LR d0 self-play. Under a lighter load v1 measured LR d2 -73% (3.75x) against hybrid4; the ratios shrink when the
  machine is busy, so compare versions only within one table.
- Exactness (v2): every game and every trained model in every test is identical to hybrid4's:
  - 4,300 flat arena games at d0, d1 and d2;
  - self-play with training at d0, d1 and d2, search labels included, and search-label training;
  - 3,080 games at d1 and d2 covering all 13 non-flat lineages (trees, the DAG, mixtures, FM, HT).
  The flat tests compared every incremental step with a full recomputation. Two runs of the same 1,000-game arena
  gave identical files. The sums are exact integers, so they are deterministic and path-independent.
- The largest remaining levers, estimated and not built (section 2):
  - the same fixed point for trees, mixtures and FM: about half the global league's tournament CPU;
  - exact vectorized LR training (the AdaGrad step and the prediction): about a quarter of global training;
  - parsing the games once for all models: about 12% of global training.

2. OPTIONS, RANKED BY CPU SAVED IN THE NEW SYSTEM
=====================================================================================================================
Reference loads are NEW-DESIGN.md's estimates at 54 models:
- the global tournament: ~163,000 games a cycle, ~4,900 CPU s a cycle;
- global training: ~7,700 CPU s a cycle, or ~9,400 with the search-label recipe;
- the stage-1 experiments: ~80 CPU-h, all flat models.

The 54 recipes are 39 trees or DAGs, 8 mixtures, 4 flat, 2 HT and 1 FM. Weighting the d2 costs above by their entries,
trees are ~74% of the tournament's CPU, mixtures ~19%, FM ~4%, HT ~1% and flat ~2%.

Exactness labels:
- IDENTICAL: same games and models as hybrid4 in every test;
- DET: deterministic and path-independent, but values can differ from hybrid4's at the rounding level, so a rare
  near-tie may play differently (games change once, then repeat);
- EXACT: bit-identical by construction.

Each option below gives its saving, its exactness and its effort.

A. Fixed point for flat LR, probit and NB, with direct values. BUILT (v1 and v2; adopt v2).
   - Saving (v2 against hybrid4, same runs): LR d2 -66%, d1 -53%, d0 -51%, d0 self-play -31%; NB d2 -67%, d1 -49%;
     probit d2 -32%, d0 self-play -10%. v1 also measured LR d4 -65% (lighter load).
   - Global league: little, since flat models are ~2% of its tournament.
   - Stage-1 experiments: their LR and NB tournaments run 2-3x faster and probit ones ~1.5x at d2. LR self-play is
     ~1.45x faster at d0 and ~3x at d2 and d4. Estimate: ~25-38 of the ~80 CPU-h (the range is machine load), most of
     it from experiment 3 (self-play depth 2 and 4).
   - Exactness: IDENTICAL in all tests (DET by construction).
   - Effort: done; merging into the tournament and self-play binaries is small.

B. Search-label training on the fixed-point search. BUILT (v1 and v2).
   - The leaf outcome is computed once per search, from the float model at the principal variation's leaf,
     instead of at every leaf.
   - Saving: train --label-depth 2 -69% on 256k positions (v1 run: 25.4 to 7.9 CPU s; v2 run: 26.8 to 8.4). That is
     ~1,200 of ~1,700 CPU s a cycle in the global league, or over 4,000 in today's league.
   - Exactness: IDENTICAL; the trained model is byte-identical (v1 and v2).

C. Direct log-odds values for every calibrated model. BUILT (v1 and v2).
   - In the search (d1 and deeper), the leaf value is z_win - z_loss from the calibrated logits, so the softmax,
     3 exp and 2 ln are skipped. d0 play is unchanged.
   - Saving (v2, 7 pairings covering all 13 non-flat lineages, single runs): d2 -2 to -14%, mean -8%; d1 about -2%,
     within the noise. v1's first runs on T-L-Ne alone gave -12 to -14%. About -8% of the global tournament,
     ~400 CPU s a cycle. Without the fixed point, LR d2 gains -17% from it (v2).
   - Exactness: IDENTICAL in 3,080 v2 games (and 80 v1 games), DET by construction.

D. Fixed point for trees, BDD, mixtures, FM and HT. NOT BUILT; the largest lever for the global league.
   - How: keep every node's sums incrementally (16 nodes x 3 outputs at split limit 5). A router picks the argmax of
     its exact sums. A mixture keeps its gate and experts' sums. FM keeps per-factor sums and squares, whose
     interaction term comes from the exact sums.
   - Estimate: -60 to -75% of model time, about -50 to -60% of a tree or mixture game and -80% for FM. That is about
     -50% of the global tournament (~2,400 CPU s a cycle).
   - Exactness: DET. A routing near-tie can go another way than in float; the flat models showed no flips in 4,300
     games (v1 and v2 each).
   - Effort: high, about 1-2 agent days.
     - TAN routers need their pairwise terms (a changed feature also changes its children's terms).
     - HT's tests are not linear, but its leaves are.

E. Parse the games once for all models (global training). NOT BUILT.
   - What: one binary cache of the batch's positions and labels (72 bytes a position), or one process training
     every recipe.
   - Saving: 0.5-0.6 us a position per model, ~12% of global training (~900 CPU s a cycle). F-L training -29%,
     F-P -62%.
   - Exactness: EXACT (same positions, same order).
   - Effort: low to moderate.

F. Vectorized LR training and prediction. NOT BUILT; needs a prototype to confirm.
   - What: store LR weights value-major (the 3 classes side by side in 4 lanes), so the AdaGrad step's sqrt and divide
     and the prediction's sums run 4 lanes at a time. Each lane does the same IEEE operations in the same order, so the
     results are bit-identical. The sums must start from -0.0 as Rust's f64 sum does, and nothing may be fused into
     FMA.
   - Saving (estimate): LR step about 2x and predict about 1.5x faster. That is LR training ~-30%, mixtures ~-35% and
     LR-leaf trees ~-20%; about -25% of global training, ~1,900 CPU s a cycle.
   - Exactness: EXACT if done as described (to be verified against hybrid4's models byte for byte).
   - Effort: moderate. The text and state formats need not change.

G. Smaller items:
   - Remaining exact fast paths from the movegen study: skipping the move text with --no-games (d0 ~-5%) and one
     legal-move list per ply at d0 (~-1.5%). Their shares grow once the model is cheap.
   - Building the fixed-point table once per round instead of once per game: ~0.5% at d0.
   - Inside the fixed-point path, the incremental update was still 36% of an LR d2 game in v1's profile. v2 trims it
     (castling only when the rights change, sums-only leaves and candidates, a linear value formula); more is
     possible.

H. Not worth it, or not exact:
   - Cheaper probit CDF math: up to ~11% of probit games; changes values.
   - NB routers' predictions from counts during training (22% of NB-router tree training): no exact shortcut,
     because every training step changes a class's denominators.
   - Eval caches: they did not pay at d<=2 (movegen study), and the fixed point makes evaluations cheaper still.
   - Skipping experts with a small gate weight: changes values; D covers mixtures exactly.

3. THE PROTOTYPE: v1 (chessai-fxlr) AND v2 (chessai-fxlr2)
=====================================================================================================================
Where things are:
- v2's source: fxlr-src/, a copy of hybrid4-src. The new module is src/fixed.rs; play_game, select, the search and the
  label search are changed; Classifier gains fixed_linear and log_odds_direct. v1's source survives only as
  fxlr-perf-src/ (v1 plus line tables and out-of-line functions for profiling).
- Binaries: see "Versions" at the top. Name history of models/from-scratch/bin/chessai-fxlr: an early v1 build was
  copied there during development, then removed; at 17:18 v2 was installed under that name; at 17:26 it was restored
  to v1 (the final v1 build, md5 0c7159d0...) and v2 was installed as chessai-fxlr2. The early build's md5 was not
  recorded, so it cannot be compared with the restored file.
- Scripts in tools/ use the same names: bin/chessai-fxlr is v1, bin/chessai-fxlr2 is v2. Logs made with v1:
  bench-fl.log, bench2.log, identity.log, identity-sp.log, identity-labels.log, arena-fx.log. Logs made with v2:
  v2.log, determinism.log, identity-kinds.log. bench3.log has both.

How it works (both versions):
- Each weight is rounded once to a multiple of 2^-32 and kept as an i64.
- A position's per-class sums are exact integers.
- A child's sums are its parent's plus the changes of the features its move changed. These are found by diffing the
  two boards' bitboards, plus the side to move, the en passant file and castling.
- Values:
  - LR and NB: v = slope_winner * (l_w - l_b) + (off_w - off_b), from the exact difference.
  - Probit: the sums are the latent's mean and variance; the CDF math is unchanged.
- The search carries the sums down the tree.
- d0 scores the candidates from their sums and encodes only the chosen move's position.
- Search outcomes (d1jit and d2jit players, search labels) come from the float model at each principal variation's
  leaf, once per search.
- v2 over v1: castling is updated only when the rights change, leaves and d0 candidates keep sums only (no encoding),
  and the LR/NB value is the linear formula above.

Controls (both versions):
- CHESSAI_FX=on (default), direct (direct values only) or off (hybrid4's arithmetic).
- CHESSAI_FX_CHECK=1 checks every incremental step against encode() and a full sum.
- A float:SPEC player plays hybrid4's arithmetic inside the same arena.

Tests: 149 unit tests pass on v2's source. That is hybrid4's 146 plus 3 new ones: random games with every child
checked, and LR, probit and NB fixed against float.

Exactness, against hybrid4 with the same seeds:
- v2, flat models (logs/v2.log), with CHESSAI_FX_CHECK=1, so every incremental step was compared with a full
  recomputation:
  - arenas, league policy: d0 LR vs probit and LR vs NB with the jitter policy, 1,000 games each; d1 LR vs probit and
    probit vs NB, 1,000 each; d2 LR vs probit and NB vs LR, 150 each. All 4,300 games and the files are identical.
  - self-play, 2 rounds, with training: LR d0, probit d0, LR d1jit, LR d2mix (search labels); and the label runs
    LR d1jit, LR d2jit, LR d2mix. Games and trained models are identical byte for byte.
  - train --label-depth 2 on 256k league positions: the model is byte-identical.
- v2, every non-flat kind (logs/identity-kinds.log, 17:23), direct values only, league policy, seed 43: 7 pairings,
  each 400 games at d1 and 40 at d2, 3,080 games in all. All files identical. The pairings cover all 13 non-flat
  lineages: T-L-Ne vs M-L4-L, M-LP-L vs T-L-Ne, FM vs T-L-H (HT), D-L-Ne (DAG) vs T-L-Te, T-L-Nc vs M-N4-L,
  M-L4-N vs T-N-Ne-merge, T-L-Le vs T-L-Ne-share.
- v2, determinism (logs/determinism.log, 17:20): the LR d2 strength arena (1,000 games, fixed point against float)

- v1: the same flat arenas, self-play and label checks (identity.log, identity-labels.log). In identity-sp.log the

4. WHAT THE USER COULD DECIDE
=====================================================================================================================
1. Adopt A + B + C, as v2 (models/from-scratch/bin/chessai-fxlr2), in the binaries before the new system launches.
   They changed no game or model in any test, so the switch would not be a break in conditions. The extra identity
   check on the field's other kinds is DONE: 3,080 games at d1 and d2 over all 13 non-flat lineages (trees, the DAG,
   mixtures, FM, HT) are identical to hybrid4's, and the determinism check passed. The fast binaries being rebuilt
   in fastbin/ do not have these changes; merging them is a separate step.
2. Whether to build D (fixed point for trees, mixtures and FM) next. It is about half the global tournament's CPU. It
   is DET: it may play differently from float in a rare near-tie. The same identity tests would measure how rare.
3. Whether to build E (parse once) and F (exact vectorized LR training). Together they are ~35% of global training
   and EXACT; F needs a short prototype to confirm its speed.
4. For experiment 3 (self-play depth 0/1/2/4), v2 roughly halves its cost now.
