Conclusion: not one LR. Unlike D-L-Nc-c, whose full model played as its leaf 3 (1 [-14, 15]), M-L4-N-c's gate splits
positions among four different LR experts, and the split is worth 46-73 Elo in play over its best expert alone and

buys is per-region calibration rather than different rankings.
