DESIGN AS BUILT (mirrors the LR residual leaves with inherit-sums; code: probit.rs "residual leaves", delegation.rs
lines marked chessai-hybrid6rp)
- The delegator's shadow is a probit model. It trains on its own prediction, on every example through the delegator,
  at the same point in the step as the LR shadow (train_shadow: after routing, before the leaf).
- A residual probit leaf holds a delta (Node::residual_probit): per-weight Gaussians (mu_d, s2_d) and its own draw
  margin. Its classifier, the summed model, has means mu_shadow + mu_d, variances s2_d and the delta's eps. That
  summed model is what predicts, is saved in model files (they load and play as plain probit trees) and is kept up to
  date (Probit::set_sum / set_sum_at) whenever the shadow or the delta trains. State files hold the deltas.
- Training a residual leaf (Probit::train_residual): exactly Probit::update with the summed model in the model's
  place (tau^2 added to the used variances, which are the delta's; the latent's truncation with the configured
  tempering; the draw-margin step), but only the delta's means, variances and eps move; the shadow's means are a fixed
  offset.
- At a split: the shadow is the leaf's model forgotten by --forget-factor (as before). New deltas have means 0 and the
  shadow's eps; with --residual-inherit-sums their variances are the shadow's widened by --split-noise, so each new
  leaf is exactly (bit for bit) the copy a split without residual leaves makes; without it their variances are the
  prior's (prior^2).
- A residual leaf that splits becomes a delegator whose shadow is its summed model (means summed, the delta's
  variances and eps), forgotten. A reset leaf gets a new delta (inheriting: the shadow's variances forgotten and
  widened, i.e. the reset copy). A merged branch below a delegator becomes a residual leaf again with delta = its
  shadow minus the shadow above (its shadow's variances and eps). Graph::forget forgets the deltas and re-syncs.
- Refused with residual probit leaves (assert in Graph::new, clear message): --residual-l2, --residual-delta-rate
  (LR delta options: a probit delta has no AdaGrad step or L2 term; its prior plays that part) and --probit-shared-eps
  (it would overwrite the summed models' margins but not the deltas').
- Departures and caveats:
  - "Its first updates equal a copy's": at the split the leaf IS the copy, and its variances, eps and step sizes stay
    exactly those of a plain probit leaf with the summed model in its place (unit test). But, as with LR residual
    leaves, the shadow trains on each example before the leaf, so the leaf's update starts from the shadow's new mean;
    a literal copy would not see that.
  - Without --residual-inherit-sums the probit leaf predicts less confidently than its shadow from the split on (the
    prior's variances enter the predictive spread), unlike LR, where AdaGrad sums don't affect predictions. Not used in
    the proposed experiment.
