THE MEASURE
- Game ids: from human.games, in the order written. Each game's move count gives its consecutive positions in its
  outcome file; the tool bails unless the counts account for every position exactly (they did for all seven
  samples).
- Halves: a splitmix64 hash of the game index (1999 and 2001 games of 4000), so both colours fall in both halves.
- Loss "cal": winner log-loss on decisive positions with the tree's log-odds recalibrated by one scale and offset,
  as `routing` (the scale and offset are fitted on all positions).
- Loss "raw": the same uncalibrated. That is the fresh gate's measure: --trial-error defaults to winner, and
  --revise-on fresh scores each round's positions with the model as it starts the round.
- Each child's loss is its subtree's prediction (the leaf its subtree routes to), as `routing`.
- Cells: (routed child, runner-up child, confidence bin); at most 12 for B = 3 (the root uses 8: some runner-up
  combinations never occur).
  - The confidence is the log-margin of the routed child's posterior over the runner-up's, split at its median over
    the router's positions. This departs from "the median of the top posterior": the NB routers' posteriors
    saturate at 1.0 in floating point (the root's median posterior printed 1.0000), so a posterior split is
    degenerate; the log-margin orders the same positions without saturating. The split points are printed per
    router: 12.3-13.3 at the roots, 1.05-4.63 below.
- G: on each half, choose m(c) = argmin_k of the summed loss of child k on cell c (ties keep the current child). Score
  the other half: G = mean over positions of loss(current child) - loss(m(c)), positive when the remapping helps.
  Both directions are pooled (each position scored once), with an SE clustered by game. The same is done at the
  B-way partition (each output group to its best child); in-sample regrets are printed beside.
- Duplicate test (cal): D(j on k's) = mean over positions routed to k of loss(j's subtree) - loss(k's subtree),
  clustered SE, both ways.
  - "Slopes only" shifts each of the two children's log-odds by its own best intercept on k's positions (1-D
    Newton), fitted on one half of the games and applied on the other.
  - A pair is DUPLICATE when both D are within 2 SE of zero.
