#   doesn't exist is skipped ("waiting for binary").

# From cycle 38 (the user, 2026-11-07, the probit agent's proposal): F-P-tau0-c, F-P-c's line plus --probit-tau 0, its
# one factor: the posterior's dynamics noise tau is 0 instead of 1e-5. Each position adds tau^2 to the variance of
# every weight it uses, so at tau 1e-5 the weights' variance floors at about 2 tau, which caps the filter's memory at
# about one round (~200k positions), and F-P-c's v1646 already sits at that floor; at tau 0 the variance keeps
# shrinking like 1/n and the model averages over all its data. The agent's brief check
# (models/from-scratch/probit/tau/summary.txt): chessai-probit from scratch, seed 21, 1000 games a round, each variant
# at the same cumulative CPU as a tau 1e-5 baseline, d2 arenas of 2 x 1000 games calibrated on heldout-lr-d2-s555,
# default jitter10. Tau 0 beat the baseline by 73 Elo at its v100, 48 at v200 and 85 (71 to 99) at v300 (94 and 76 on
# the two seeds); tau 1e-6 by 48, 47 and 63. A larger tau loses badly: 1e-4 by 415 and 421, 1e-3 by 512 and 561 (a
# control confirmed the arena: the baseline against itself 10, and with the sides swapped 389, mirroring the 415). The
# model files store tau, so the own step's train --start keeps it at 0; the own step trains and gates with
# chessai-probit (own_games.binary_by_lineage in config.json), as F-P-c's. Risk: at tau 0 the model keeps stiffening
# over the league's 1000+ rounds, so late own steps may barely move it; tau 1e-6 is the hedge. The league decides.
